title: "Why We Chose a Faux-Leather Sofa for Our First Home"
description: "I loved the black faux-leather sofa I saw in the showroom. We ordered ours in ivory, about 2.9 m wide."
---

I loved the black faux-leather sofa at the Monocus showroom in I’Park Mall. My fiancé liked the goose-filled cushions. The manager said the upholstery would hold up well to scratches from cats or dogs. We chose ivory for ours. It’s about 2.9 m wide.

Our robot vacuum doesn’t fit under the sofa we ordered.
