title: "Why We Chose a Faux-Leather Sofa for Our First Home"
description: "My fiancée loved the black faux-leather sofa in the showroom. We ordered ours in ivory, about 2.9 m wide."
---

My fiancée loved the sofa in the showroom. The one on display was black faux leather and had a look all its own. I liked the goose-filled cushions, too. The manager told us the upholstery would hold up well to scratches from cats or dogs. We ordered ours in ivory. It’s about 2.9 m wide.

We found it at the Monocus showroom in I’Park Mall. Our robot vacuum doesn’t fit under the sofa we ordered.
