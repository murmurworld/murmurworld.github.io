---

I started out looking for a light-colored modular sofa in fabric. IKEA’s JÄTTEBO caught my eye because I could take off the off-white bouclé cover and wash it. Then we sat on the Monocus Leel at the I’Park Mall showroom. I still wanted a light color. After that visit, I was leaning toward leather and goose-feather cushions. [IKEA’s JÄTTEBO cover](https://www.ikea.com/kr/ko/p/jaettebo-cover-1-5-seat-module-with-storage-axvall-off-white-50622370/) · [Monocus Leel](https://monocus.co.kr/product/?idx=3)

## We were keeping the dining table

The sofa had to leave enough room to pull out our dining chairs. We were aiming for about two meters of width, and the standard three-seat Leel we looked at was well over that. I also wanted to choose the leg height so our robot vacuum could get underneath. **We went custom so we could choose both the width and the clearance.**

I’m looking for the overall width, the clearance under the sofa, and the cancellation and return terms on the custom quote.
