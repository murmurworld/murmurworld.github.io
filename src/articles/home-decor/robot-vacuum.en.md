We ordered the Samsung Bespoke AI Steam Ultra with four other appliances at the department store. The Ecovacs DEEBOT T50 PRO OMNI was the first one I looked into. Before I opened any spec sheets, I had a question: would it get under the bed?

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Robot height</p>
  <h2>I used 12 cm as a starting point</h2>
  <p>I wanted the vacuum to reach under the bed and sofa, so I wrote down the heights first. With a hypothetical 120 mm of open space all the way under the furniture, the 81 mm Ecovacs would have 39 mm to spare and the 100 mm Samsung would have 20 mm. Once the furniture is in place, I’ll measure at the lowest part of the frame, including the center support. If a rug is in the way, I’ll measure from its surface too.</p>
  <p>I also looked at where the sensor sits. The T50 PRO OMNI’s distance sensor is built into its body. The Dreame X60 Ultra has a sensor that rises from the top. That puts it at 79.5 mm with the sensor down and 102.8 mm with it up. The sensor adds over two centimeters, so I wrote down both measurements.</p>
</section>

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Dock</p>
  <h2>The dock washes the pads. I empty the tank.</h2>
  <p>Our Samsung dock empties the robot’s dustbin and washes, steams and dries the mop pads. I’ll refill the clean-water tank and empty the dirty-water tank myself. There’s also a similarly named model that connects to a water supply and drain. The model number helped me tell them apart.</p>
  <p>Samsung lists the robot and dock together at 444 mm wide, 520 mm high and 510 mm deep. If I put it beside a cabinet, I’ll also check that I can lift the tanks out, reach an outlet and leave the robot room to turn in front of the dock.</p>
</section>

## What I’ll check once the furniture is in

- The lowest point along the robot’s path under the bed and sofa, including any center supports
- Rug edges, door thresholds and charging cables on the floor
- Room in front for the robot to dock and room above to lift out the tanks
