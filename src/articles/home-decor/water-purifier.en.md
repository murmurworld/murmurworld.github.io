Ice at the press of a button sounded handy. Once I thought about looking after the ice bin and the parts that make the ice, a water-only model began to make more sense for us. We looked at a narrow Coway unit and an LG with a hot-water sanitizing cycle. The Samsung we ordered with the rest of our appliances has filters we can change ourselves and a spout we can take off and wash.

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Ice</p>
  <h2>We could make our own ice</h2>
  <p>Samsung’s ice-making model is 24&nbsp;cm wide. The purifier we bought is 17&nbsp;cm wide. Saving 7&nbsp;cm of counter space suited us. We could get ice from the freezer when we needed it.</p>
</section>

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Upkeep</p>
  <h2>What the machine does and what I clean</h2>
  <p>This model connects to the water supply and filters water as it dispenses it. The unit runs its own cycles for the tubing I can’t reach, and I can remove and wash the end of the spout.</p>
  <div class="guide-panel">
    <h3>What Samsung lists</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Water line</strong> · An electrolytic sanitizing cycle runs every three days. When the unit is idle, it drains leftover water from the line every four hours.</li>
      <li><strong>Spout</strong> · The tip comes off for washing.</li>
      <li><strong>Filters</strong> · The HAF-HIM set has two cartridges. The recommended interval is 12 months, and the replacement alert means it’s time to change both.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <p class="guide-point"><strong>Takeaway</strong><span>I checked what the machine handles automatically and what I can reach and wash myself.</span></p>
</section>

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Installation</p>
  <h2>A narrow purifier can still be deep</h2>
  <p>It’s 17&nbsp;cm wide and 48&nbsp;cm deep. From the front it looks slim, but it extends farther back than the Coway and LG models we compared. Samsung also lists holes in the counter for installation, so we looked at where it would sit and how the supply and drain lines would reach it.</p>
</section>
