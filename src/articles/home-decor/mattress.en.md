We tried mattresses together before choosing one. When we first started looking, we knew we wanted something on the softer side. Lying down on a few in stores helped us put a finger on it. We both liked the Sealy feel, with a cushioned top and firm support underneath.

In August, we spent about ten minutes lying on a mattress at the store before ordering a **Sealy Chante II queen**. It measures 150 × 200 cm. Sealy lists this model as available in stores. [Sealy Chante II](https://sealy.co.kr/product/chante-ii-%EC%83%B9%EB%96%BC-ii-%EB%A7%A4%ED%8A%B8%EB%A6%AC%EC%8A%A4/4397/)

Delivery was one of the first differences we spotted. The Zinus and 3boon1 models come compressed in a box. The Sealy Miles Elite arrives at full size. All three are queens, but they need different amounts of room to get through a front door or lift.
