title: "Why We Chose a 609 L Kitchen Fit Max Fridge After Considering an 834 L Model"
description: "We compared depth, width, freezer capacity, and monthly electricity use with a kimchi refrigerator in mind."
---

After our next move, I want to put a kimchi fridge beside the main one. We’d first picked the 834 L LG **S834MQQ111** for its roomy freezer, light finish, and grade 1 efficiency rating. The fridge changed to a **609 L Kitchen Fit Max** when we bought five appliances together at a Samsung shop inside a department store.

The Samsung was **23 cm shallower** and only **1 mm narrower**. What mattered to me was how far it would stick out into the kitchen with both appliances side by side.

## How the two fridges compared

| What we compared | LG S834MQQ111 | Samsung RM80H64S2A |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total capacity (manufacturer / agency) | 832 L / 834 L | 609 L / 611.5 L |
| Freezer capacity | 308 L | 233 L |
| Width × height × depth | 913 × 1,790 × 913 mm | 912 × 1,853 × 683 mm |
| Korean energy efficiency grade | Grade 1 | Grade 2 |
| Monthly electricity use (manufacturer) | 40.2 kWh | 38.5 kWh |
| Monthly electricity use (agency listing) | 37.95 kWh | 36.13 kWh |

The manufacturers and the Korea Energy Agency list different capacities for the same models, so I used the model numbers to match them up. Samsung’s dimensions exclude the rear handle. [LG specifications](https://www.lge.co.kr/product/refrigerators/s834mqq111) · [Samsung specifications](https://www.samsung.com/sec/support/model/RM80H64S2A/) · [Agency listing for LG](https://eep.energy.or.kr/certification/certi_view_252.aspx?no=252250342&page=1) · [Agency listing for Samsung](https://eep.energy.or.kr/certification/certi_view_252.aspx?no=252260150&page=1)

We went from a 308 L freezer to 233 L. The electricity figures were less than 2 kWh a month apart on either basis when I compared the manufacturers’ figures with each other and the agency figures with each other. I used Samsung’s 38.5 kWh a month in our [home appliance electricity calculator](/en/blog/home/decor/power/).

## Making room for one fridge, then two

Samsung’s installation example for this fridge shows 4 mm on each side and 20 mm above. With the 912 mm fridge, that makes the opening in the example 920 mm wide. Once we move and choose the kimchi fridge, we’ll measure for both appliances, including clearance to swing the doors open and pull their drawers out.
