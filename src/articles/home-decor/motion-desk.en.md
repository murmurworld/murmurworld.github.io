We decided to take turns using one desk and keep one monitor for now. The desk we chose for our place is the 140 cm DESKER Premium Motion Desk.

## Saved heights for two people

When two people share a desk, the height that feels right changes along with the person using it. We can each save a height with our hands on the keyboard while sitting, then get back to it at the press of a button. The DESKER Premium stores four heights, enough for each of us to save a sitting and a standing position. [DESKER’s height adjustment guide](https://www.desker.co.kr/news/detail/22)

The desks also differed in how high they could go. The Fiture E2 was the least expensive option. The IKEA MITTZON offered an 80 cm-deep top and two presets for just under ₩500,000. If you want to fit a monitor and a laptop on the same desk, check the depth as well as the width.

## Before fitting the monitor arm

The clamp has to fit the thickness of the desktop, and the screw underneath needs room to clear the frame and cable tray. I’ll check that gap before fitting our monitor arm.

Leave enough slack in the power and monitor cables at the desk’s highest setting. Cables that hang loose while you’re sitting can pull taut when the desk goes up. [DESKER Premium user manual](https://cdn.desker.co.kr/_upload/manual/2211/F1667290247276QUHUMHQODBQ.pdf)
