We started out shopping online, picking from a mix of brands. I’d settled on a light-colored LG fridge and was looking at Samsung for laundry. Samsung was also our pick for the water purifier, while the robot vacuum was going to be a different brand. Then a Samsung shop inside a department store gave us a quote for all five appliances together. The quote was good enough to make us change the lineup. Even the fridge I’d picked out ended up changing.

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">The order</p>
  <h2>From the fridge to the air&nbsp;purifier</h2>
  <p>We’d looked at each appliance on its own online. Once we got the bundle quote, all five came from Samsung. The apartment already had two ceiling-mounted AC units, so we left air conditioning off the order.</p>
  <div class="guide-panel">
    <h3>What we ordered together</h3>
    <p>Fridge, washer-dryer, water purifier, robot vacuum, and air purifier.</p>
  </div>
  <p class="guide-point"><strong>Takeaway</strong><span>We kept the AC units the apartment came with and ordered the five appliances we still needed.</span></p>
</section>

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Fridge</p>
  <h2>Room for two fridges&nbsp;later</h2>
  <p>I’d almost decided on the light-colored 834 L LG with a grade 1 efficiency rating. The bundle quote led us to the 609 L Samsung Kitchen Fit Max. It held less but was 23 cm shallower. We wanted to put a kimchi fridge beside it when we moved, so I also thought about how far the two would stick out into the kitchen.</p>
  <div class="guide-panel">
    <h3>Monthly electricity use on the same basis</h3>
    <p>The Korea Energy Agency lists the LG at 37.95 kWh a month and the Samsung at 36.13 kWh. The difference between these two models was under 2 kWh a month.</p>
    <p><a href="/en/blog/home/decor/refrigerator/">Why we chose the 609 L Kitchen Fit Max over the 834 L fridge</a></p>
  </div>
  <p class="guide-point"><strong>Takeaway</strong><span>The fridge we chose had less storage and a shallower body.</span></p>
</section>

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Laundry</p>
  <h2>One machine in the laundry&nbsp;room</h2>
  <p>The first plan was to put a dryer on top of the washer. The laundry room was a little short for that, so we went with a combo that washes and dries in one drum. I especially liked that the door opens by itself after drying.</p>
  <div class="guide-panel">
    <h3>The combo we chose</h3>
    <p>Samsung Bespoke AI Laundry Combo WD90H25AHY. It takes up to 25 kg for washing and 20 kg for drying.</p>
    <p><a href="/en/blog/home/decor/washer-dryer/">Why we chose a washer-dryer combo</a></p>
  </div>
  <p class="guide-point"><strong>Takeaway</strong><span>One machine saved us having to fit two units vertically.</span></p>
</section>
