title: "Why We Bought Our Appliances at a Department Store"
description: "We started with a mix of brands and ended up buying five Samsung appliances together at a department store."
---

We bought our fridge, washer-dryer, water purifier, robot vacuum, and air purifier together at a Samsung shop inside a department store. We’d started with an LG fridge, a Samsung washer-dryer and water purifier, and a robot vacuum from another brand on our online shortlist. By the time we got a quote for all five together, our order looked quite different from the list we started with.

## We’d settled on an 834 L LG first

For a while, the 834 L LG was at the top of the list. It came in the light color we wanted and had a grade 1 efficiency rating. The fridge changed to a 609 L Kitchen Fit Max when we chose the Samsung bundle. We wanted room for a kimchi fridge beside it in our next home, so we liked that the Samsung was 23 cm shallower. [LG specifications](https://www.lge.co.kr/product/refrigerators/s834mqq111) · [Samsung specifications](https://www.samsung.com/sec/support/model/RM80H64S2A/)

The Korea Energy Agency lists the LG at 37.95 kWh a month and the Samsung at 36.13 kWh. The manufacturers’ figures put the Samsung slightly lower too, and the gap was under 2 kWh a month on either basis. [Agency listing for LG](https://eep.energy.or.kr/certification/certi_view_252.aspx?no=252250342&page=1) · [Agency listing for Samsung](https://eep.energy.or.kr/certification/certi_view_252.aspx?no=252260150&page=1)

## One machine for washing and drying

I’d first planned to stack a dryer on top of the washer. The laundry room was a little short for that, so we went with a combo that takes 25 kg for washing and 20 kg for drying. I especially liked that the door opens on its own when drying is done. We left air conditioning off the quote because there were already two ceiling units in the apartment. [Samsung washer-dryer guide](https://www.samsung.com/sec/laundry-combo/combo-wd90h25ahy-d2c/WD90H25AHY/)
