I started with the idea of putting a dryer on top of the washer. Our laundry room was a little short for that, so I started looking at combos that wash and dry in one drum. When we got a quote at the department store, I liked the model with a door that opens on its own after drying. That’s the one we ordered with the rest of the appliances.

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Capacity</p>
  <h2>One drum, two&nbsp;limits</h2>
  <p>A combo washes and dries in the same drum. Ours can wash up to 25 kg and dry up to 20 kg. The smaller number tells you how much can go through a full wash-and-dry cycle in one load.</p>
  <div class="guide-panel">
    <h3>Same name, different drying capacities</h3>
    <p>The 2024 model in the photo dries up to 15 kg, while the 2026 model we ordered dries up to 20 kg. Both are called the Bespoke AI Laundry Combo. We looked at the model number alongside the capacity.</p>
  </div>
  <p class="guide-point"><strong>Takeaway</strong><span>Drying capacity is the limit when washing and drying a single load.</span></p>
</section>

<section class="guide-section">
  <p class="guide-label">Auto-open door</p>
  <h2>The door opens when the drying is&nbsp;done</h2>
  <p>I liked knowing I could leave the laundry in the same drum from start to finish. At the shop, the feature that stood out was the door opening by itself. On the model we ordered, you can choose when it opens in the machine’s settings.</p>
  <div class="guide-panel">
    <h3>Auto-open settings</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Open as soon as the cycle ends</li>
      <li>Open five minutes after the cycle ends</li>
      <li>Turn automatic opening off</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="guide-note">These are the settings Samsung lists for the WD90H25AHY.</p>
  </div>
  <p class="guide-point"><strong>Takeaway</strong><span>Look up auto-open availability and settings by model number.</span></p>
</section>
