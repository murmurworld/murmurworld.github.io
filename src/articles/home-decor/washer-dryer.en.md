I’d originally planned to stack a dryer on top of the washer. Our laundry room was a little short for two machines stacked that way. When we bought five appliances together at the department store, we chose the **Samsung Bespoke AI Laundry Combo WD90H25AHY**, which washes and dries in one drum.

I liked that the door can open by itself when drying is done. On this model it can open as soon as the cycle ends or five minutes later, and you can turn the feature off altogether. [Samsung’s automatic door guide](https://www.samsung.com/sec/laundry-combo/combo-wd90h25ahy-d2c/WD90H25AHY/)

## One machine to get into the laundry room

The model we chose takes **25 kg for washing** and **20 kg for drying**. One drum, two limits. The unit is **686 mm wide, 1,110 mm high, and 875 mm deep**, and it weighs 145 kg. Choosing one machine settled the height question. Getting it through the laundry room door and over the threshold is still a job in itself.
