We started by looking for a trash can that could seal the bag for us. The Townew T Air X made the list, so we looked up its refills and how we would put the trash out. We ended up with a 27 L Magikan Hippo that we received as a gift. Its pedal opens the outer and inner lids together. [Magikan Hippo guide](https://company.magikan.co.kr/contact/faq)

## Opening the lid and taking out the trash

A sensor lid opens when you move a hand close to it. With a self-sealing bin, you press a button to heat-seal the bag when you empty the bin. The Townew T Air X also fits a new bag after you remove the sealed one. [Townew T Air X product guide](https://townewonline.ca/products/t-air-x)

## Once the bag comes out

Seoul asks residents to put general waste out in designated municipal bags, called *jongnyangje* bags. If you use a Townew bag or Magikan liner for general waste, put the filled liner inside a municipal bag for collection. With a sensor bin that takes the municipal bag directly, you tie that bag and put it out. [Seoul’s waste disposal guide](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/finf/elsi/wast/contPageDetail.do?conts_no=9B31E123320D4935BD07B1D413DF0035)

Match the refill to the model label on the Hippo. A pack of five 280R5B rolls costs about ₩17,000. Townew lists up to 25 bags from one refill ring for the T Air X. How often you empty the bin and which refills it takes can make as much difference as the price on the bin itself.
