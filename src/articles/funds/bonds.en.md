<section><h2>A rolling maturity bucket</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/bonds/shy/">SHY</a> and <a href="/en/blog/funds/bonds/tlt/">TLT</a> both own U.S. Treasury debt. Bonds roll out as their remaining lives leave the benchmark window. Individual bonds therefore have a repayment date, while a share of either ETF has no corresponding maturity.</p>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/bonds/vwob/">VWOB</a> also holds dollar obligations, but its issuers are emerging sovereigns and government-related bodies. Movements in U.S. Treasury rates and changes in how the market assesses each borrower both affect the bonds.</p></section>
