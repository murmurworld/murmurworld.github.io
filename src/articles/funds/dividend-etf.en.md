<section><h2>Selecting dividends and writing options</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/dividend-etf/schd/">SCHD</a> and <a href="/en/blog/funds/dividend-etf/ace-us-dividend-dow-jones/">ACE US Dividend Dow Jones</a> use a Dow Jones benchmark family that screens U.S. dividend history and fundamentals. <a href="/en/blog/funds/dividend-etf/vig/">VIG</a> looks for a long sequence of annual increases, while <a href="/en/blog/funds/dividend-etf/dgro/">DGRO</a> considers growth and payout capacity.</p>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/dividend-etf/kodex-200-weekly-covered-call/">KODEX 200 Target Weekly Covered Call</a> sells call options, giving another party the right associated with buying the index on agreed terms. The fund receives a premium and gives up part of the upside on its written exposure. That changes the possible sources of distributions.</p></section>
