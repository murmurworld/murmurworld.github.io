<section><h2>Bullion custody, futures rolls and direct ownership</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/gld/">GLD</a>, <a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/iaum/">IAUM</a> and <a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/slv/">SLV</a> hold metal in custody and their shares represent interests in a trust. <a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/ace-krx-gold/">ACE KRX Gold Spot</a> connects to the Korean spot market, introducing won translation and local trading conditions.</p>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/cper/">CPER</a> can hold copper futures with different delivery dates. Rolling from one month into another introduces a contract spread. Direct ownership of <a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/bitcoin/">BTC</a> instead depends on the ability to authorize spending with the relevant keys.</p></section>
