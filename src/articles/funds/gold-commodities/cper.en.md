<p class="lead">CPER is an exchange-traded commodity pool built around copper futures. Its portfolio changes delivery months rather than storing a permanent inventory of physical copper.</p>

<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The SummerHaven Copper Index Total Return selects one or three eligible copper futures maturities each month under rules tied to the futures curve. Its total-return design also includes a collateral component.</p>
<p>The prospectus describes copper futures plus cash or Treasuries supporting the contracts. Delivery months roll and the portfolio has no fixed list of mining companies. 2026-04-24 · <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001479247/000207187626000122/i26205_cper-424b3.htm">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>Futures require margin rather than full cash payment of the contract’s notional amount. CPER holds collateral in cash, cash equivalents or Treasuries. It is a commodity pool subject to CFTC regulation.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Rolling replaces an expiring contract with a later maturity. Differences across delivery months, collateral income and operating expenses can separate the pool’s path from spot copper.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/sector-etf/copx/">COPX</a> holds mining companies. CPER holds copper futures, making the spread between contract months a direct factor alongside copper itself.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Find the Investment Objective and the index description for contract selection. Fees and Expenses lists cost components. Risk Factors addresses the futures curve and collateral. Delivery month identifies the actual contract.</p></section>
