<p class="lead">IAUM is a U.S. trust holding physical gold. “Micro” describes the share format. Its underlying gold market is the same bullion market used by much larger trusts.</p>

<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The reference for bullion valuation is the LBMA Gold Price PM. Relating a trust share to an ounce requires the metal entitlement per share and the expenses accruing against assets.</p>
<p>The quarterly schedule identifies bullion as the investment asset. Gold per share can change as trust expenses are paid. 2026-06-30 · <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001759124/000143774926026003/iaum20260630_10q.htm">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The trust holds bars and changes its inventory through authorized-participant basket transactions. The financial statements reconcile metal added or removed and gold sold to pay expenses.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>The stated sponsor fee is 0.09% annually, with a voluntary reduction to 0.07% through June 30, 2027. The trust discloses that the sponsor can terminate the reduction with advance notice.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/gld/">GLD</a> also holds bullion. Each trust discloses its own amount of gold per share and its own fee schedule.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Read the investment schedule for bullion. Item 1 Business of the annual report covers custody, creation and redemption. Item 1A Risk Factors covers custody and trading exposure. The Sponsor’s Fee note explains how expenses are funded.</p></section>
