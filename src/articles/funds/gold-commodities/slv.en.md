<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The trust documents specify the LBMA Silver Price for valuation. Bullion is measured in troy ounces, and the silver entitlement attached to one share can change.</p>
<p>The year-end investment schedule reports physical silver. The trust holding relates to bullion, as distinct from a mining business or a long-dated futures position. 2025-12-31 · <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1330568/000143774926006059/slv20251231_10k.htm">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>Authorized participants can deposit silver to create baskets of shares and redeem baskets in the other direction. Public investors trade shares on the exchange. Custody and secondary trading are separate functions.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Because silver pays no interest, sponsor fees can reduce the metal held per share. The annual report identifies the custodian, arrangements with subcustodians and physical custody risks.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p>Like <a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/gld/">GLD</a>, SLV stores a physical metal. Industrial uses such as solar panels and electronics give silver a different demand mix.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Read the investment schedule for bullion. Item 1 Business of the annual report covers custody, creation and redemption. Item 1A Risk Factors covers custody and trading exposure. The Sponsor’s Fee note explains how expenses are funded.</p></section>
