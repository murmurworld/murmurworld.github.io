<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The KRX Gold Spot Index reflects won-denominated trading in the domestic gold market. Global bullion prices, exchange rates and domestic trading conditions can all affect that reference.</p>
<p>The manager listed one spot asset at 100%, KRX 1 kg gold at 99.99% purity. The kilogram describes the exchange bullion specification. Each ETF share represents a fraction of the fund. 2026-11-26 · <a href="https://papi.aceetf.co.kr/api/funds/K55101DN7441/pdf?page=0&amp;size=10&amp;std_dt=20261126">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The portfolio holds domestic spot gold and cash used for settlement. An exchange buyer receives fund units, while physical delivery and the underlying gold accounts belong to a different trading arrangement.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Trading in won leaves the economic effect of global gold and the dollar intact. A Korean spot-market fixing and a London benchmark refer to different times and venues.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/gld/">GLD</a> and <a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/iaum/">IAUM</a> are U.S.-listed gold trusts valued in dollars. The Korean spot ETF also reflects won translation and local spot-market conditions.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>In Part II of the Korean prospectus, the objective and strategy identify the benchmark and permitted assets. Investment risks covers tracking and applicable currency exposure. Check both the stated fee and total expense figures, then the trust agreement for limits and payment terms.</p></section>
