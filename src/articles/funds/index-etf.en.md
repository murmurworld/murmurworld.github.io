<section><h2>Reading the benchmark and mandate</h2>
<p>Start with index eligibility. The S&amp;P 500 selects eligible large U.S. companies, while the Nasdaq-100 selects large non-financial companies listed on Nasdaq. Listing and sector rules change the portfolio even within U.S. equities.</p>
<p>Look next at direct holdings. <a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/voo/">VOO</a> holds company shares. <a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/aoa/">AOA</a> holds iShares equity and bond ETFs. The three TIME funds use their indices as reference points for active portfolios, so actual positions and weights can diverge.</p></section>
