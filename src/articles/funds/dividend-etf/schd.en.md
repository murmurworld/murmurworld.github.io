<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Index applies ten-year payment history, size and liquidity tests. Annual selection then weighs cash flow relative to debt, return on equity, dividends and dividend growth.</p>
<p>Fund weights included <a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/procter-gamble/">Procter &amp; Gamble</a> at 4.64%, <a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/home-depot/">Home Depot</a> at 4.48% and <a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/coca-cola/">Coca-Cola</a> at 4.37%. 2026-11-25 · <a href="https://www.schwabassetmanagement.com/products/schd">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The fund holds U.S. stocks. Quarterly reweighting limits individual names to 4% and sectors to 25% at the adjustment. Market moves between reviews can push an actual holding above its original cap.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Company dividends first flow into the fund. Its distributions to shareholders reflect those receipts and fund expenses, so the dollar payment can vary from one period to another.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/dividend-etf/ace-us-dividend-dow-jones/">ACE US Dividend Dow Jones</a> brings the same U.S. dividend benchmark family to Korea, trading in won. Trading currency, operating expenses and dividend processing can differ.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
