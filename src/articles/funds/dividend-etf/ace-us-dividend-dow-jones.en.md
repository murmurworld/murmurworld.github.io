<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>Its benchmark converts the Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Price Return Index into won. The methodology screens a long history of dividend payments and evaluates financial strength alongside dividend characteristics. Dow Jones reviews the constituent list annually and rebalances weights quarterly.</p>
<p>Korea Investment Management’s own fund basket listed <a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/procter-gamble/">Procter &amp; Gamble</a> at 4.70%, <a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/home-depot/">Home Depot</a> at 4.46% and <a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/coca-cola/">Coca-Cola</a> at 4.44%. 2026-11-26 · <a href="https://papi.aceetf.co.kr/api/funds/K55101DN4471/pdf?page=0&amp;size=10&amp;std_dt=20261126">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The fund mainly holds U.S. dividend stocks and receives the dividends those companies declare. The won-denominated price index and cash distributions are separate lines in the product documents.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>The policy leaves exchange-rate exposure unhedged and sets monthly distribution dates. Won translation and ex-distribution adjustments can affect reported values at different times.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/dividend-etf/schd/">SCHD</a> shares the U.S. stock selection rules. ACE trades in Korean won, but its dollar equity exposure still brings currency translation.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>In Part II of the Korean prospectus, the objective and strategy identify the benchmark and permitted assets. Investment risks covers tracking and applicable currency exposure. Check both the stated fee and total expense figures, then the trust agreement for limits and payment terms.</p></section>
