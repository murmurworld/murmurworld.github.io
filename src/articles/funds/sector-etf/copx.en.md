<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The Solactive Global Copper Miners Total Return Index screens companies globally for copper-mining exposure. Business and revenue eligibility are reviewed in its semiannual reconstitution and rebalance.</p>
<p>The annual report listed <a href="/en/blog/companies/industry-energy-materials/southern-copper/">Southern Copper</a> at 5.0% and Freeport-McMoRan at 4.7% of net assets. These are portfolio weights, distinct from the miners’ share of copper production. 2025-10-31 · <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1432353/000093041326000079/0000930413-26-000079.txt">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The usual policy invests at least 80% of assets in index securities, including applicable depositary receipts. The portfolio’s mining shares do not entitle the ETF shareholder to delivery of copper metal.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Ore grades, output, local currencies and labor relations all affect the underlying companies. These exposures distinguish mining shares from a strategy that rolls copper futures.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/gold-commodities/cper/">CPER</a> rolls copper futures. COPX adds mine operating costs, production rates, financing and capital-spending decisions to copper exposure.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
