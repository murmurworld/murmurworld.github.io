<p class="lead">TLT holds long-dated U.S. Treasuries. The issuer is the same as for shorter Treasury funds, but long-dated cash flows can be much more sensitive to interest rates.</p>

<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The ICE U.S. Treasury 20+ Year Bond Index targets Treasuries with more than twenty years to maturity. Bonds that age beyond eligibility can leave at a subsequent index review. ICE updates index holdings on the last calendar day of each month.</p>
<p>The two largest Treasury-bond positions were 4.31% and 4.11%. Each issue has its own remaining term. 2026-09-30 · <a href="https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/fact-sheet/tlt-ishares-20-year-treasury-bond-etf-fund-fact-sheet-en-us.pdf">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The manager uses representative sampling. Its stated policies allocate at least 90% of assets to U.S. Treasuries and at least 80% to securities in the index. Issues with different coupons and maturity dates can coexist.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Discounting cash flows far into the future amplifies the effect of rate changes. The fund continually refreshes its long-maturity exposure, unlike one Treasury held to a known redemption date.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/bonds/shy/">SHY</a> selects one- to three-year maturities. TLT&#x27;s distant payments are discounted over a longer period, increasing sensitivity to long-term rates.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
