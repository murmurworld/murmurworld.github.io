<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>Bloomberg screens eligible emerging-market government-related dollar debt by criteria including maturity and issue size. Concentration limits constrain issuer weights within the eligible market. The index provider rebalances the portfolio monthly.</p>
<p>Country exposures included Saudi Arabia at 13.7%, Mexico at 10.8% and Türkiye at 6.2%. Their eligible bonds in this index are denominated in U.S. dollars. 2026-09-30 · <a href="https://fund-docs.vanguard.com/F3820.pdf">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>Vanguard samples the index across credit quality, maturities and issuers. A state-related company can be eligible, so its relationship to a government is not enough to establish a sovereign guarantee.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>U.S. dollar interest rates and issuer-specific credit spreads both matter. A government collecting local-currency revenue may find dollar obligations harder to service when its currency weakens.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/bonds/tlt/">TLT</a> concentrates on the U.S. Treasury. VWOB owns bonds across emerging sovereign and government-related issuers, adding country-specific credit exposure.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
