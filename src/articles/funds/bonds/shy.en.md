<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>ICE selects dollar-denominated fixed-rate U.S. Treasuries by remaining term. A note originally issued for much longer can enter the short-term band as its redemption date approaches. ICE updates eligible issues on the last calendar day of each month.</p>
<p>The two largest Treasury-note issues represented 2.10% and 1.99%. A single issuer can have many bonds with different maturities and coupons. 2026-09-30 · <a href="https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/fact-sheet/shy-ishares-1-3-year-treasury-bond-etf-fund-fact-sheet-en-us.pdf">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The fund holds Treasury securities and samples the index to approximate maturity and interest-rate exposure. Coupon cash flows into the fund, with cash balances available for settlement and distributions.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Aging securities leave the maturity band as other notes enter. Duration approximates sensitivity to interest-rate changes and is distinct from the time remaining to principal repayment.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/bonds/tlt/">TLT</a> also holds U.S. government debt. SHY has much shorter remaining terms, changing its sensitivity to the same move in yields.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
