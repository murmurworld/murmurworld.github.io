<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The Nasdaq-100 selects 100 large non-financial companies listed on Nasdaq. Its modified capitalization weighting includes rules that limit concentration at rebalancing. From 2026, certain large newly listed companies qualify for fast entry, and quarterly reviews can replace lower-ranked constituents.</p>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/qqqm/">QQQM</a>, a conventional tracker of the same index, held <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/apple/">Apple</a>, <a href="/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/nvidia/">NVIDIA</a> and <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/microsoft/">Microsoft</a>. QLD reports its derivative and collateral positions separately. 2026-11-24 · <a href="https://www.invesco.com/us/en/financial-products/etfs/invesco-nasdaq-100-etf.html">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The fund can combine index stocks, swaps with financial institutions and futures. Short-term instruments used alongside derivatives also appear in the holdings list.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>For illustration, an index rising 10% and then falling 10% ends at 99% of its starting level. A perfect daily double before costs ends at 1.20 × 0.80, or 96%. Both daily moves affect the result.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/leveraged/tqqq/">TQQQ</a> targets three times the same index’s daily change. <a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/qqqm/">QQQM</a> tracks the underlying index without a daily leverage reset.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
