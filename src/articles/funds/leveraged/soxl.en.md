<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>ICE&#x27;s NYSE Semiconductor Index selects 30 large eligible U.S.-listed semiconductor companies. Modified capitalization weights give the constituents different influence. ICE reconstitutes the index annually and rebalances it quarterly.</p>
<p>Benchmark weights included <a href="/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/intel/">Intel</a> at 9.61%, <a href="/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/amd/">AMD</a> at 9.29% and <a href="/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/micron/">Micron</a> at 7.95%. These describe the index constituents. 2026-09-30 · <a href="https://www.direxion.com/product/daily-semiconductor-bull-bear-3x-etfs">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>Swaps and other derivatives, index securities or an ETF tracking the benchmark can be used to obtain daily exposure. The portfolio can adjust contracts as the index and target exposure change.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Chip demand and capital spending can move much of this sector at once. Daily resets, swap counterparties and moves around the close add other sources of divergence.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p>SOXX tracks the same NYSE Semiconductor Index without a multiplier. SMH uses a separate index of 25 U.S.-listed semiconductor companies. <a href="/en/blog/funds/leveraged/koru/">KORU</a> has a similar daily target but a different underlying market.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
