<section aria-labelledby="h-t-index" class="read">
<h2 id="h-t-index">The benchmark</h2>
<p>The Nasdaq-100 selects 100 of the largest eligible non-financial companies listed on Nasdaq, including non-U.S. issuers. Nasdaq uses modified market-cap weighting, reconstitutes the index in December and reviews weights quarterly.</p>
<p><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/apple/">Apple</a>, <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/microsoft/">Microsoft</a> and <a href="/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/nvidia/">Nvidia</a> illustrate the kinds of businesses represented in the Nasdaq-100. Nasdaq publishes the list and weights separately from the fund’s holdings.</p>
<p>Under rules effective in May 2026, certain large newly listed companies can enter early. Starting in June, quarterly reviews can replace constituents that fall far enough in rank. <a href="https://indexes.nasdaqomx.com/docs/2026_NDX_Changes_FAQ.pdf">Nasdaq methodology update</a></p><p>For an illustration from an ordinary tracker of this index, on 2026-11-24 QQQM held Apple at 9.09%, Nvidia at 6.59% and Microsoft at 6.49%. TQQQ reports its own swaps and collateral separately. <a href="https://www.invesco.com/us/en/financial-products/etfs/invesco-nasdaq-100-etf.html">Invesco holdings</a></p></section>

<section aria-labelledby="h-t-how" class="read">
<h2 id="h-t-how">How TQQQ obtains threefold exposure</h2>
<p>Under normal circumstances at least 80% of assets are invested in index securities or instruments with similar economic characteristics. The fund can use three kinds of instruments.</p>
<div class="prod">
<div><h3>Stocks</h3><p>The fund can own common shares of companies in the index.</p></div>
<div><h3>Swaps</h3><p>Financial institutions agree to exchange cash flows based on the index and a financing rate over a specified term. The contract can run from a day to more than a year.</p></div>
<div><h3>Futures</h3><p>Standardized futures contracts provide Nasdaq-100 exposure with margin posted as collateral.</p></div>
</div>
<p>Cash held alongside derivatives can be invested in short-term instruments such as U.S. Treasury bills maturing within one year.</p>
</section>

<section aria-labelledby="h-t-daily" class="read">
<h2 id="h-t-daily">One day versus several days</h2>
<p>A 1% rise in the index on a given day translates to a 3% daily objective. The next day begins with a different fund asset base. Each daily change therefore compounds on the previous one.</p>
<p>Hypothetical calculation before expenses. An index that rises 10% and falls about 9.09% is back at its start. Applying three times each daily move takes the fund from 100 to 130 and then to approximately 94.5.</p>
<p>The prospectus warns of a possible total loss if the index approaches a 33% decline intraday. A swap counterparty’s failure to meet its obligations creates a separate risk.</p>
</section>
