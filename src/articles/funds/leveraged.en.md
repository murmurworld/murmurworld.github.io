<div class="how">
<h3>How the fund builds exposure</h3>
<p>The funds can combine stocks, index futures and swaps with financial institutions to adjust exposure to their daily targets. Cash held alongside derivatives may be invested in short-term Treasury bills.</p>

<section aria-labelledby="h-daily" class="read">
<h2 id="h-daily">What a one-day target means</h2>
<p>Each product resets daily. After several sessions the sequence of index moves matters. Consider a hypothetical index that rises one day and falls back to its starting point the next.</p>
<div aria-label="Worked two-day example" class="calc" role="table">
<div class="h" role="columnheader"></div><div class="h" role="columnheader">Index</div><div class="h" role="columnheader">Daily 3× fund</div>
<div role="rowheader">Start</div><div>100</div><div>100</div>
<div role="rowheader">First day, index +10%</div><div>110</div><div>130</div>
<div role="rowheader">Second day, index −9.09%</div><div class="k">100</div><div class="k">94.5</div>
</div>
<p class="note">Hypothetical calculation before fees and expenses. The fund drops about 27.27% on day two. Multiplying 130 by 0.7273 gives about 94.5. The fund ends about 5.5% below its starting value.</p>
<p>The prospectus warns that an intraday index decline approaching one third could exhaust a 3× fund’s value.</p>
</section>
