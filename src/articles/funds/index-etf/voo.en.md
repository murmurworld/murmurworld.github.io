<p class="lead">One VOO share gives exposure to a portfolio of large U.S. companies. Their weights depend on the size of each company’s publicly tradable shares.</p>

<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>An S&amp;P Dow Jones Indices committee applies size, liquidity and profitability criteria. Float-adjusted market capitalization determines weights. Quarterly updates and events such as acquisitions can change the portfolio.</p>
<p>Portfolio weights included <a href="/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/nvidia/">NVIDIA</a> at 8.4%, <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/apple/">Apple</a> at 7.4% and <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/microsoft/">Microsoft</a> at 5.8%. 2026-09-30 · <a href="https://fund-docs.vanguard.com/F0968.pdf">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>Vanguard uses full replication, holding index stocks in approximately their index weights and collecting their dividends. The ETF is a share class of a larger index fund. Figures for the entire fund can include other classes.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>Holding 500 companies does not divide the fund into 500 equal slices. A move in one of its largest constituents can matter more than moves in several smaller ones.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/qqqm/">QQQM</a> draws from large Nasdaq-listed non-financials. VOO&#x27;s S&amp;P 500 can also hold banks such as <a href="/en/blog/companies/financials/jpmorgan/">JPMorgan Chase</a>.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
