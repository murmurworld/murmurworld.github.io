<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The reference is the Nasdaq-100 price index translated into won. The manager describes a baseline 60:40 split between benchmark-related and actively selected positions, allowing active weights to differ.</p>
<p>The manager reported <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/apple/">Apple</a> at 7.66%, <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/microsoft/">Microsoft</a> at 7.35% and <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/amazon/">Amazon</a> at 7.02%, a snapshot of this active strategy. 2026-11-25 · <a href="https://timeetf.co.kr/m11_view.php?idx=2&amp;cate=001">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The ETF holds overseas equities. Its largest corporate holdings may overlap the benchmark, but the manager’s allocations and trading dates determine the fund weights.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>The fund does not hedge its underlying currency exposure. Korean exchange hours can fall outside U.S. cash-market hours, so trading time and the timing of underlying equity prices can differ.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/qqqm/">QQQM</a> seeks to match the Nasdaq-100. TIME can assign different company weights relative to its U.S. equity benchmark.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>In Part II of the Korean prospectus, the objective and strategy identify the benchmark and permitted assets. Investment risks covers tracking and applicable currency exposure. Check both the stated fee and total expense figures, then the trust agreement for limits and payment terms.</p></section>
