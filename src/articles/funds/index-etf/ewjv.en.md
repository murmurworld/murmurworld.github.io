<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>MSCI assesses value characteristics using book value, forward earnings and dividends relative to share prices. On October 13, 2026, the manager reported 32.63% in financials and 18.86% in industrials.</p>
<p>Fund holdings included Mitsubishi UFJ Financial at 9.17%, <a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/toyota/">Toyota</a> at 6.41% and Sumitomo Mitsui Financial at 5.85%. 2026-09-30 · <a href="https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/fact-sheet/ewjv-ishares-msci-japan-value-etf-fund-fact-sheet-en-us.pdf">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>BlackRock may use a representative sample of stocks matching the index’s risk and sector characteristics. Japanese markets close before U.S. valuation time, so fair-value adjustments can enter the fund’s NAV.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>The strategy does not seek to hedge the yen. Even unchanged local equity values translate differently when the yen-dollar rate changes. The value screen can also shift sector weights away from the broad Japanese market.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/voo/">VOO</a> covers large U.S. stocks. EWJV first takes a Japanese large- and mid-cap universe and then applies value signals.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
