<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>The benchmark translates Bloomberg’s global large-, mid- and small-cap equity price index into won. The strategy starts from a 60:40 broad-market and sector allocation and reviews positions monthly.</p>
<p>The manager&#x27;s basket included SPDR Portfolio MSCI Global Stock Market ETF at 28.41%, Vanguard Total World Stock ETF at 28.36% and iShares MSCI ACWI ETF at 27.77%. 2026-11-25 · <a href="https://timeetf.co.kr/m11_view.php?idx=22&amp;cate=001">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>Broad and sector funds can both own the same business. To measure indirect exposure, multiply each underlying ETF’s portfolio weight by the company’s weight inside it, then add across ETFs.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>The prospectus distinguishes 0.23% in fund-level annual fees and expenses from approximately 0.40% including estimated underlying fund costs. The strategy does not hedge foreign currencies.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/aoa/">AOA</a> also owns other ETFs. AOA follows a benchmark with a bond allocation, while TIME&#x27;s manager chooses allocations against a global equity benchmark.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>In Part II of the Korean prospectus, the objective and strategy identify the benchmark and permitted assets. Investment risks covers tracking and applicable currency exposure. Check both the stated fee and total expense figures, then the trust agreement for limits and payment terms.</p></section>
