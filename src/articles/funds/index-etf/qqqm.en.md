<section><h2>The underlying exposure</h2>
<p>Nasdaq selects eligible issuers and uses modified market-cap weights. Membership is reviewed annually in December and weights quarterly. Multiple share classes can make the security count exceed the company count. From 2026, certain large newly listed companies qualify for fast entry, and quarterly reviews can replace lower-ranked constituents.</p>
<p>Fund weights included <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/apple/">Apple</a> at 9.09%, <a href="/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/nvidia/">NVIDIA</a> at 6.59% and <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/microsoft/">Microsoft</a> at 6.49%. 2026-11-24 · <a href="https://www.invesco.com/us/en/financial-products/etfs/invesco-nasdaq-100-etf.html">Composition source</a></p></section>

<section><h2>What the product holds</h2>
<p>The fund invests at least 90% of its total assets in index securities. Its holdings file shows actual share classes and portfolio weights, while the index rulebook explains eligibility.</p></section>

<section><h2>How it behaves</h2>
<p>The portfolio follows the ordinary index through its securities. The non-financial and large-company concentrations follow directly from eligibility rather than a manager’s view of those industries.</p></section>

<section><h2>How related products differ</h2>
<p><a href="/en/blog/funds/index-etf/time-us-nasdaq100-active/">TIME US Nasdaq 100 Active</a> can depart from Nasdaq-100 weights. <a href="/en/blog/funds/leveraged/qld/">QLD</a> and <a href="/en/blog/funds/leveraged/tqqq/">TQQQ</a> instead target twice and three times the daily move of the same index.</p></section>

<section><h2>Where to look in the documents</h2>
<p>Investment Objective defines the target. Fees and Expenses identifies annual costs and any waivers. Principal Investment Strategies explains the benchmark and instruments. Read Principal Risks alongside the holdings to identify the source of each exposure.</p></section>
