Flip a light switch and the electricity comes in through the local distribution network. Further upstream are transmission lines that carry power long distances and substations that change the voltage along the way. Companies that sell generators and companies that sell grid equipment belong to the same industry because they work at different points along that path. [U.S. Energy Information Administration · Electricity delivery](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/delivery-to-consumers.php)

The organizations running the grid and the companies delivering transformers or batteries work at different points along that path. ## What a power plant buys

A gas plant burns fuel with compressed air. The hot gases spin a turbine, which drives a generator. A wind turbine uses moving air to drive its generator. GE Vernova makes both types of turbine. A buyer compares different constraints for each project, including how to supply the fuel, what the local wind is like, and how much output varies over time. [U.S. Energy Information Administration · Electricity generation](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/how-electricity-is-generated.php) · [GE Vernova · How a gas turbine works](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/resources/education/what-is-a-gas-turbine)

For a large gas plant on a 60 Hz grid, buyers might compare GE Vernova’s 7HA with Siemens Energy’s SGT6-9000HL. Check whether the published output is gross or net of the plant’s own electricity use before comparing the numbers. Maintenance intervals and the time the plant has to shut down for service also matter to the contract. [GE Vernova · 7HA](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/gas-turbines/7ha) · [Siemens Energy · HL-class turbines](https://www.siemens-energy.com/global/en/home/products-services/product-offerings/gas-turbines/hl-class.html)

A **combined-cycle** plant uses the gas turbine’s exhaust heat to make steam and turn a second turbine. Check whether a bid gives the output of the gas turbine alone or includes the steam turbine as part of the whole plant. [Mitsubishi Power · combined-cycle power plant](https://power.mhi.com/products/gtcc/)

## Transformers and grid batteries

At a higher voltage, the same power can travel at a lower current, reducing losses on the line. A substation’s **transformers** change the voltage. Other equipment connects and disconnects power lines as needed. Utilities and grid operators buy this equipment when they connect plants or expand transmission capacity. [U.S. Energy Information Administration · Transformers](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/batteries-circuits-and-transformers.php) · [GE Vernova 2025 Annual Report](https://www.gevernova.com/sites/default/files/gevernova_2025_annual_report.pdf)

Tesla’s **Megapack** is a large battery system. It can charge when electricity is plentiful and send it back later. A battery could charge during a sunny afternoon and discharge in the evening. **MW** measures how much power it can deliver at a time. **MWh** measures the energy it can store. Together they give an idea of how long the battery can deliver that output. [Tesla · Megapack](https://www.tesla.com/megapack) · [U.S. Energy Information Administration · Electricity glossary](https://www.eia.gov/tools/glossary/index.php?id=electricity)

Imagine a **20 MW, 80 MWh battery**. If all of that energy is usable, it can deliver rated power for about four hours. In Korea, a government plan calls for batteries on the Honam distribution network to store surplus renewable electricity. When a project announcement gives output in MW, look for how long the system is designed to deliver it. [NREL and USAID battery storage FAQ](https://www.sandia.gov/app/uploads/sites/256/2024/02/Grid-Scale-Battery-Storage-Frequently-Asked-Questions.pdf) · [Korean government’s Honam storage plan](https://www.mcee.go.kr/home/web/board/read.do?boardId=1876770&boardMasterId=939&menuId=10598)

## Separating equipment and services in the filings

GE Vernova’s 2025 annual report has separate sections for `Power`, `Wind` and `Electrification`. In `Power`, compare `Equipment` with `Services` to see the relative size of the original sale and the business after installation. Tesla’s `Energy Generation and Storage` line in its 2025 10-K includes residential Powerwall and energy generation as well as Megapack. When you look up battery deployments, check whether the company is reporting energy capacity in GWh or revenue in dollars. [GE Vernova 2025 Annual Report](https://www.gevernova.com/sites/default/files/gevernova_2025_annual_report.pdf) · [Tesla 2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm)
