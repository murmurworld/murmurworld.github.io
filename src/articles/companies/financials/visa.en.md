## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| VisaNet | The network carries authorization and clearing information between the merchant's and cardholder's institutions, then facilitates settlement. |
| Cross-border transactions | Visa provides services when an issuer and merchant are in different countries, with possible currency conversion. Cross-border volume and total payment volume cover different activity. |
| Value-added services | Risk management, issuing and acceptance solutions, advisory and other services supplement transaction processing. |

## Who buys it

Financial institutions, processors, merchants and partners use the network. The cardholder usually borrows from the issuing bank rather than the network operator.

Visa reported one client at 11% of total net revenue in each of the fiscal years ended September 2026, 2025 and 2024. The filing did not identify the client. The percentage applies to Visa net revenue, not payments volume. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000134/v-20260930.htm)

## Where to look in the filing
