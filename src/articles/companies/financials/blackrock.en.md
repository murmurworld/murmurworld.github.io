## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Investment advisory and fund administration | BlackRock manages active, index and cash strategies including iShares ETFs. Base fees depend on mandates and assets under management. |
| Technology services | Aladdin supplies portfolio and risk technology. Preqin provides private-market data and analytics to professional users. |
| Performance fees | Certain mandates provide for additional fees tied to performance terms. This revenue differs from recurring base fees. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Period-end assets under management reached USD 15.5 trillion. Net inflows during the quarter were USD 205 billion. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312526420979/blk-ex99_1.htm) |

## Who buys it

Pension funds, institutions, financial advisers and individuals invest through mandates and funds. Institutions also license technology.

Pension plans and insurers can hire BlackRock to manage assets for a fee. The annual report also names such institutions among its technology-service clients. Buying portfolio management and buying an Aladdin subscription create different revenue streams for BlackRock. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312526071966/blk-20251231.htm)
