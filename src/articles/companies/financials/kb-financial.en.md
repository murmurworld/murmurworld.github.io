## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Banking | KB Kookmin Bank takes deposits and makes loans to households and businesses. Net interest income is stated after funding interest expense. |
| Securities | KB Securities supplies brokerage, wealth services and capital raising or advisory work for companies. |
| Insurance | Life and non-life insurers issue policies, collect premiums and meet claims. Their results depend on underwriting and investment activity. |
| Cards | KB Kookmin Card earns fees and financing income from payments and card lending. Default costs are distinct from transaction processing costs. |

## Where to look in the filing

In the DART business report, Business Overview sets out principal products, services and sales. The consolidated notes cover segments and major customers. Interim income statements distinguish the three-month column from year to date.

The segment tables distinguish operating profit from net income. Consolidation adjustments include intragroup transactions. The measure in each heading governs comparisons across banking, brokerage and insurance.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
