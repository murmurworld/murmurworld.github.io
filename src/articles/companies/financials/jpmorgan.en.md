## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Consumer and Community Banking | Chase supplies deposits, cards, mortgages and other consumer or small-business products. A lending balance generates interest and exposes the bank to credit costs. |
| Commercial and Investment Bank | Companies and institutions use lending, capital markets, trading and securities services. Underwriting and advisory fees differ from interest on a loan. |
| Asset and Wealth Management | The bank manages investments and offers advice and banking to affluent clients and institutions. |
| Payments | Treasury and merchant clients pay for money movement and transaction processing. These activities sit inside the wider banking segments. |

## Who buys it

Households, businesses, institutions and government bodies borrow, deposit, make payments, raise capital or entrust assets to the group.

JPMorgan Chase sells distinct services to cardholders and corporate clients. Card use creates payment activity and can generate lending income. A company hiring the investment bank for an issuance or merger can pay an advisory or underwriting fee. The entire purchase charged to a card is not bank revenue. ## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

Net interest income deducts the cost of funding from interest on loans and securities. No operating margin is calculated for periods without a separately reported operating income figure.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
