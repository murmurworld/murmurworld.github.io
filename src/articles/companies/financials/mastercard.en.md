Mastercard processes payment messages between participating banks and supplies fraud, data and other value-added services. Issuers and acquirers connect consumers and merchants to the network. Banks, merchants and governments can also buy analytical or security services.

## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Payment network | Network services route payment information and support authorization, clearing and settlement. Mastercard generally does not extend the cardholder credit. |
| Value-added services and solutions | Authentication, security, loyalty, insights and consulting help customers manage their payment businesses. |
| Account-to-account payments | Infrastructure can also move funds between bank accounts, separate from the traditional card transaction path. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |

## Who buys it

Issuers and acquirers connect consumers and merchants to the network. Banks, merchants and governments can also buy analytical or security services.

No individual customer exceeded 10% of net revenue in 2023–2025. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1141391/000114139126000013/ma-20251231.htm)
