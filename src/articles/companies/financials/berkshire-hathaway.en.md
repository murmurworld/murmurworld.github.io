## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Insurance | Primary insurers and reinsurers accept risks in exchange for premiums. Underwriting results depend on claims and expenses as well as premium volume. |
| BNSF Railway | Shippers pay to move agricultural, industrial, consumer and other freight over a rail network. |
| Berkshire Hathaway Energy | Utilities, pipelines and other energy operations earn revenue under a mix of regulated and commercial arrangements. |
| Manufacturing, service and retail | The group owns a range of industrial, consumer, service and distribution businesses. Equity investments are reported separately from these subsidiaries' operations. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q2 | Berkshire reported USD 12.983 billion of operating earnings under its own definition. Net earnings attributable to shareholders were USD 25.667 billion and include investment gains and losses. [Company results or filing](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/aug0826.pdf) |

## Who buys it

The operating businesses serve policyholders, freight shippers, utility users and buyers of industrial or consumer products. Insurance investments are distinct from the premiums customers pay.

Berkshire’s operating companies serve different customers. Lubrizol reports that some of its major buyers can also be suppliers. No single customer exceeded 10% of Lubrizol’s 2025 revenue. The disclosure refers specifically to that subsidiary. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067983/000119312526083899/brka-20251231.htm)

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
