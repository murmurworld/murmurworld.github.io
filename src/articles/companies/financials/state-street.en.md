## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Investment servicing | The group safeguards assets, keeps investment records, values portfolios and supplies trading or related services. Custody assets remain clients' property. |
| Investment management | Index and active strategies, including SPDR ETFs, generate management fees. |
| Net interest income | Client deposits and the assets funded with them generate a spread. Assets under custody and assets under management are different measures and must not be added. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Assets under custody and/or administration totaled USD 59.2 trillion. Assets under management totaled USD 6.3 trillion. Their service scopes overlap. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93751/000009375126000592/a3q26earningspressrelease.htm) |

## Who buys it

Institutional investors and asset managers outsource custody or administration. Investors can separately buy funds from its investment management business.

Funds, pension plans, insurers and investment managers hire State Street for custody and servicing. The value of securities they entrust to the bank enters its custody figures. Fees for accounting and settlement support enter revenue. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93751/000009375126000124/stt-20251231.htm)
