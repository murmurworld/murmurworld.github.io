## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| USDC and reserve income | USDC is designed to be redeemable for US dollars. Assets held in reserve generate income, and partner distribution arrangements affect how much Circle retains. |
| Circle Mint | Eligible institutional customers can exchange fiat currency for supported stablecoins and redeem back into fiat under the applicable terms. |
| Arc and developer tools | Network and software infrastructure supports developers building payment applications. A product's development stage matters when reading current revenue. |
| Circle Payments Network | Participating institutions use network services to facilitate cross-border settlement. Transfer volume is not the same as Circle's revenue. |

## Who buys it

Institutions use Circle for issuance, redemption and payment infrastructure. Reserve income depends on the assets backing stablecoins, with distribution costs paid under partner arrangements.

## Its place in the sector

Circle issues and redeems USDC. External partners help manage the reserve fund and distribute the stablecoin.

Buyer [Circle](/en/blog/companies/financials/circle/) reserve fund · Supplier [BlackRock](/en/blog/companies/financials/blackrock/). Management of the government money market fund holding a portion of USDC reserves. The same filing identifies BNY as custodian. [Circle 2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1876042/000187604226000062/crcl-20251231.htm)

Buyer [Circle](/en/blog/companies/financials/circle/) · Supplier Coinbase. Distribution payments under an agreement that takes reserve income and balances held on each platform into account. [Circle 2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1876042/000187604226000062/crcl-20251231.htm)

## Where to look in the filing
