<h2>One jet engine creates two routes for revenue</h2>

<p>An airframer needs an engine when it builds an aircraft. Airbus lists CFM International’s LEAP-1A and Pratt &amp; Whitney’s GTF as A320neo-family options. CFM is the joint venture of <a href="/en/blog/companies/industry-energy-materials/ge-aerospace/">GE Aerospace</a> and Safran, while Pratt &amp; Whitney is part of <a href="/en/blog/companies/industry-energy-materials/rtx/">RTX</a>. Two possible suppliers do not turn one engine delivery into revenue for both.</p>

<p>After delivery, the engine accumulates flight hours. Airlines and maintenance providers buy replacement parts or book shop visits. This year’s service revenue can come from engines delivered years ago. A similar distinction between new equipment and service on the installed base matters when reading power-equipment filings.</p>
