A wafer arrives at a chip fab as a smooth, polished disc of silicon. A materials supplier such as SUMCO cuts and polishes the disc. A foundry builds circuits on it from a customer’s design, using equipment from suppliers such as ASML. Following the wafer from the materials supplier to the fab makes the companies’ roles easier to see. [SUMCO wafer manufacturing](https://www.sumcosi.com/english/products/process/)

## Fabless companies design. Foundries manufacture.

A fab is a factory that processes semiconductor wafers. A **fabless** company such as NVIDIA designs chips and contracts out wafer manufacturing and other production steps. A **foundry** such as TSMC turns customers’ designs into chips on wafers. TSMC also offers advanced packaging. For example, its CoWoS process connects a compute chip and high-bandwidth memory, or HBM, within one package. Samsung Foundry is another option for customers considering contract production. Buyers check design support, delivery times, yield, and packaging requirements even when process generations carry similar labels. [NVIDIA FY2026 filing, Manufacturing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm) · [TSMC 2025 annual report, CoWoS](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/annual-report/2025/2025%20TSMC%20Annual%20Report.E.pdf#page=52) · [Samsung Foundry](https://semiconductor.samsung.com/foundry/)

## The machines behind one wafer layer

One way to make a layer is to **apply a material → transfer a pattern with light → remove selected material → check the result**. Fabs repeat these operations to build up layers. At some points, chemical reactions and mechanical polishing flatten the surface before more layers go on. This step is called CMP. [ASML chipmaking guide](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/all-about-microchips/how-microchips-are-made) · [Applied Materials on CMP](https://www.appliedmaterials.com/in/en/semiconductor/products/shape/cmp.html)

- **Deposition** coats the wafer with a thin film of material. Applied Materials sells deposition equipment and tools for other steps, including polishing, etching, and measurement. [Applied Materials FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6951/000162828025056742/amat-20251026.htm)
- **Lithography** uses light to transfer a circuit pattern onto a photosensitive coating. ASML supplies deep ultraviolet (DUV) and extreme ultraviolet (EUV) systems. The resulting pattern helps determine which material is removed in a later step. [ASML lithography principles](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles)
- **Etching** removes selected material, leaving the features defined by the pattern. Lam Research sells etching, deposition, and cleaning tools. Its product range overlaps with Applied Materials in some areas. A buyer evaluates tools for the same process and material. [Lam Research FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/707549/000070754926000037/lrcx-20260628.htm)
- **Inspection and metrology** find defects and measure features at several stages. KLA sells inspection and measurement systems and software that analyzes process data. A fab can use those results to adjust how it processes later wafers. [KLA FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/319201/000031920126000027/klac-20260630.htm)

## Compare lithography tools for the same job

ASML’s TWINSCAN NXT:2100i and Nikon’s NSR-S636E are both ArF immersion DUV scanners. **Overlay** measures how far a new pattern is misaligned with features on a previous layer. A fab checks how many wafers per hour a scanner can handle while meeting the alignment target. Compare the published figures using the wafer diameter, exposure conditions, and how each manufacturer measured alignment. [ASML specifications](https://www.asml.com/en/products/duv-lithography-systems/twinscan-nxt2100i) · [Nikon product announcement](https://www.nikon.com/company/news/2023/1206_01/)

## Follow what the fab pays for

In its 2025 annual report, TSMC says **3-nanometer technology accounted for 24% of wafer revenue**. That figure groups revenue by process generation. The separate platform breakdown shows applications such as smartphones and high-performance computing (HPC). [TSMC 2025 annual report](https://investor.tsmc.com/static/annualReports/2025/english/index.html)

ASML’s 2025 report separates system sales from **service and field options** for installed systems. Lam Research also separates new system sales and customer support-related revenue in its annual filing for the year ended June 28, 2026. Its support category includes services, parts, upgrades, and some equipment sales. Keeping orders, recognized system sales, and support revenue separate helps you see what fabs buy to build a line and what they pay to keep it running. [ASML 2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm) · [Lam Research FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/707549/000070754926000037/lrcx-20260628.htm)
