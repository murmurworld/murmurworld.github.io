Say a new stock joins the index an ETF tracks. The manager decides how much to buy. The custodian records the fund’s new holding once the trade settles. A dollar-linked digital token also divides up the work. Circle issues USDC, and BlackRock manages the fund that holds part of its reserves. [KB Kookmin Bank’s guide to custody](https://obiz.kbstar.com/quics?page=C017022) · [Circle’s reserve disclosures](https://www.circle.com/transparency)

## BlackRock manages investment portfolios

**BlackRock (BLK)** manages iShares ETFs, other funds and portfolios for institutional clients. For an index product it manages holdings against a benchmark. For an active strategy it makes investment decisions within the mandate it agreed with clients. BlackRock typically earns management fees as a percentage of **assets under management**, or AUM. Certain portfolios can also earn performance fees, and Aladdin adds technology and subscription revenue. [BlackRock FY2025 Form 10‑K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312526071966/blk-20251231.htm)

BlackRock reported **$14.0 trillion** in AUM at the end of 2025. Net inflows for the year were about **$698 billion**, and market gains added about **$1.5 trillion**. Acquisitions and exchange rates also affect the total. Separating new client money from gains on assets already invested helps explain why AUM grew. [BlackRock FY2025 Form 10‑K on AUM](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312526071966/blk-20251231.htm)

## State Street looks after assets and fund operations

When a fund buys shares, someone has to confirm settlement, keep a record of the holdings and process events such as dividends. **State Street (STT)** charges for services including custody, accounting and fund administration. It reports the value of assets covered by those activities as **assets under custody and/or administration**, or AUC/A. The mix of assets and services and the terms of client contracts help explain servicing fees. [State Street FY2025 Form 10‑K on servicing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93751/000009375126000124/stt-20251231.htm)

State Street also manages investments, including ETFs. At the end of 2025 it reported **$53.80 trillion** in AUC/A and **$5.67 trillion** in AUM. Read the first alongside servicing fees and the second alongside management fees. The filing also reports net interest income from its banking activities. [State Street FY2025 Form 10‑K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93751/000009375126000124/stt-20251231.htm)

BlackRock’s **IVV** and State Street’s **SPY** track the same S&P 500 index. This is where the two companies compete directly in fund management. Put the fund documents side by side to compare how closely each tracks the index, the expenses it charges, and how it trades. [iShares IVV](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239726/ishares-core-sp-500-etf) · [State Street SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy)

## Circle issues USDC and earns income on reserves

**Circle (CRCL)** issues USDC, a stablecoin designed so that one unit tracks the value of one US dollar. Circle says it holds dollar-denominated reserves for USDC separately from its operating funds. The backing includes cash and short-dated US Treasury assets. BlackRock manages the **Circle Reserve Fund**, which holds part of those reserves. This is a case where one business issues the token and another manages part of the backing. [Circle’s reserve disclosures](https://www.circle.com/transparency)

Circle reported about **$2.64 billion** of reserve income and **$1.66 billion** of distribution and transaction costs for fiscal 2025. Reserve balances and interest rates influence reserve income. Costs include amounts paid to partners that distribute the token. Reading those lines together shows how much of the reserve income is offset by distribution and transaction costs. [Circle FY2025 Form 10‑K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1876042/000187604226000062/crcl-20251231.htm)

## Where to start in the filings

| Company | What shows the size of the activity | What shows its revenue or costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BlackRock | Year-end AUM and net inflows during the year | Base fees and performance fees |
| State Street | Year-end AUC/A and AUM | Servicing fees and management fees |
| Circle | Reserve balances and composition | Reserve income and distribution and transaction costs |

A year-end asset balance describes a particular day. Management fees and reserve income add up over the year, so check both the date and the reporting period when reading the figures.
