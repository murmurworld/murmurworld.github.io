A game might play an ad for another game before you start the next round. The advertiser wants to reach potential players, and the developer of the game you are playing has space to sell. The two businesses meet on a screen you see for a few seconds.

<h2 id="ad-auction">Before the Ad Appears</h2>

In auction-based advertising, eligible ads compete whenever a slot becomes available. Google Search uses factors such as the search query, bid and quality of the ad and landing page to rank candidates. Meta also runs an auction when an ad can be shown, taking into account how likely people are to respond and the quality of the ad. [Google Ads on Ad Rank](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1752122) · [Meta on ad auctions](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-auction)

Search engines can show ads where people look for web pages. Retailers can show them where shoppers look for products. **Amazon Sponsored Products** and South Korea’s **Coupang Product Ads** both help sellers promote products. Both charge for clicks on the ads. [Amazon Sponsored Products guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/sponsored-products-best-practices) · [Coupang Product Ads guide in Korean](https://ads.coupang.com/blog/61)

<h2 id="ad-billing">What does the advertiser pay for?</h2>

Suppose the game ad **appears 1,000 times, gets 20 clicks, and is credited with four installs**. All three events can be counted in one campaign. [TikTok’s bidding methods](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/bidding-methods?lang=en) · [Branch’s explanation of cost per install](https://www.branch.io/glossary/cpi-cost-per-install/)

A campaign billed for clicks can still report the cost per install. The product and contract tell you which events are billed. The campaign report tells you what results followed.

## Where App Advertising Platforms Fit

When the developer puts a slot up for sale, ad networks can bid to fill it. AppLovin’s **MAX** helps the publisher run that competition. South Korea’s **AdPopcorn Monetize** also connects app publishers to several networks for slots such as video and banner ads. [AppLovin December 2025 10-K, Business](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1751008/000175100826000010/app-20251231.htm) · [AdPopcorn Monetize in Korean](https://www.adpopcorn.com/publishers/monetize/)

AppLovin’s **Axon Ads Manager** helps advertisers find places to buy and manage their campaigns. Its 2025 filing says Axon Ads Manager generated most of its revenue. The filing also explains that MAX revenue is based on a percentage of client spending. [AppLovin December 2025 10-K, Business and results discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1751008/000175100826000010/app-20251231.htm)

## Reading Advertising Revenue in Filings

- **Alphabet** splits Google advertising into Google Search & other, YouTube ads, and Google Network. The YouTube advertising line is separately visible. Search advertising shares a line with other advertising sources. [December 2025 10-K, financial statement Note 2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000018/goog-20251231.htm)
- **Meta** groups advertising on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and third-party apps in its reported advertising revenue. Its results discussion also includes ad impressions and average revenue per ad. [December 2025 10-K, results and financial statement Notes 2 and 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026003942/meta-20251231.htm)
- **Amazon** groups sponsored, display and video ads in advertising services. Sponsored Products illustrates one way it sells ads. The filing’s revenue line covers a wider set of services. [December 2025 10-K, financial statement Note 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm)
- **AppLovin** sold its own apps business in 2025. The annual filing shows that business under discontinued operations and reports the remaining business, including ad tools such as MAX, as a single segment. [December 2025 10-K, financial statement Notes 3 and 14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1751008/000175100826000010/app-20251231.htm)
