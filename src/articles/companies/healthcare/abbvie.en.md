## Who buys it

Wholesalers and distributors buy medicines, while aesthetics products also reach specialist practices. Payers affect net realized revenue through coverage and rebates.

AbbVie says McKesson, Cardinal Health and Cencora accounted for substantially all of its U.S. pharmaceutical product sales in 2025. No wholesaler represented more than 43% of U.S. gross revenues. That domestic upper bound does not give an individual distributor’s share of global sales. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000008/abbv-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Lilly and Novo overlap in diabetes and obesity. J&J sells both medicines and devices. AbbVie and Merck market multiple lines including immunology and oncology products.

Companies in this group · [Eli Lilly](/en/blog/companies/healthcare/eli-lilly/) · [Novo Nordisk](/en/blog/companies/healthcare/novo-nordisk/) · [Johnson & Johnson](/en/blog/companies/healthcare/johnson-johnson/) · [Merck & Co.](/en/blog/companies/healthcare/merck/)

Buyer McKesson, Cardinal Health and Cencora · Supplier [AbbVie](/en/blog/companies/healthcare/abbvie/). US pharmaceutical wholesale distribution. The 2025 filing names all three counterparties. [AbbVie 2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000008/abbv-20251231.htm)

Buyer Janssen ([Johnson & Johnson](/en/blog/companies/healthcare/johnson-johnson/) group, outside the US) · Supplier Pharmacyclics ([AbbVie](/en/blog/companies/healthcare/abbvie/) group). Collaboration and license for Imbruvica: Janssen has exclusive commercialization rights outside the US and shares pretax profits and losses under the agreement. [AbbVie 2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000008/abbv-20251231.htm)

Adalimumab biologics · The FDA identifies Amgen’s Amjevita as a biosimilar to Humira from [AbbVie](/en/blog/companies/healthcare/abbvie/).. [FDA · Amjevita](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761024s027lbl.pdf) · [AbbVie 2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000008/abbv-20251231.htm)
