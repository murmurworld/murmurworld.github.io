## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Diabetes and obesity | Mounjaro and Zepbound are brand names for tirzepatide with different approved uses. Product revenue is reported by brand and geography. |
| Oncology | Cancer medicines include Verzenio and other products. Sales depend on approved indications and access, in addition to the number of patients. |
| Immunology | Medicines in this area address immune-mediated disorders. Examples include Taltz and other products listed in the annual report. |
| Neuroscience and other therapies | The group also sells neuroscience and other medicines, including Kisunla. A label or approval describes the eligible patient population, not revenue. |

## Its place in the sector

Lilly and Novo overlap in diabetes and obesity. J&J sells both medicines and devices. AbbVie and Merck market multiple lines including immunology and oncology products.

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
