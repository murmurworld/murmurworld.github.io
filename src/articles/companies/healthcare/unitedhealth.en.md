## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| UnitedHealthcare | Health benefit plans receive premiums or service fees. Claims and medical costs determine how much of premium revenue is available for other uses. |
| Optum Health | Provider and care-delivery businesses serve patients under fee-based and risk-bearing arrangements. |
| Optum Rx | Pharmacy benefit management, dispensing and related services support health plans and plan sponsors. |
| Optum Insight | Data, software, analytics and administrative services support healthcare organizations. Intersegment sales must be eliminated at the consolidated level. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | The medical care ratio was 88.4%. It measures medical costs as a share of premium revenue. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176626000255/earningsrelease3q26unh.htm) |

UnitedHealthcare buys services from Optum, its sister business. Optum records those sales in its own revenue. In the third quarter of 2026, UnitedHealthcare reported $86.330 billion in revenue and Optum reported $66.883 billion. Adding the two and subtracting $40.519 billion in transactions within the group gives the $112.694 billion in consolidated revenue that UnitedHealth Group reported. [UnitedHealth Group third-quarter earnings release](https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/content/dam/UHG/PDF/investors/2026/unh-reports-third-quarter-2026-results.pdf)

## Who buys it

Employers, public programs and individuals buy health benefits. Providers, payers and plan sponsors also contract for Optum services.

Premiums from the U.S. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services represented 44% of UnitedHealth Group’s consolidated 2025 revenue. Most arose in UnitedHealthcare Medicare & Retirement. The figure identifies the payer of the premiums, rather than the number of people receiving care. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731766/000073176626000062/unh-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector
