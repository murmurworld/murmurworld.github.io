## Who buys it

Drug wholesalers, healthcare providers and public purchasers buy human medicines or vaccines. Veterinary and agricultural customers buy animal health products.

Merck supplies medicines through wholesalers including McKesson, Cencora and Cardinal Health. Its 2025 year-end figures of 22%, 21% and 13% for these companies measure shares of outstanding accounts receivable. They do not measure shares of annual sales. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/310158/000031015826000063/mrk-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Lilly and Novo overlap in diabetes and obesity. J&J sells both medicines and devices. AbbVie and Merck market multiple lines including immunology and oncology products.
