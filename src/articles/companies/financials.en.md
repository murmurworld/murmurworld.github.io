<h2>What happens when a card is tapped?</h2>

<p>The authorization request travels from the terminal through the acquirer and network to the issuer. The issuer checks the account and returns a response. An approval at the till does not mean settlement between banks has already taken place.</p>

<p>After clearing, the institutions settle and the merchant receives its proceeds under its acquiring agreement. Network fees, interchange accruing to the issuer and any cardholder interest belong to different parties. The purchase amount processed is much larger than the network’s own revenue.</p>
