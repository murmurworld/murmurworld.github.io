## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Search advertising | An advertiser can bid for an opportunity to display an ad next to a search result. Revenue recognition depends on the type of ad transaction. |
| YouTube | Advertisers fund ad-supported viewing and subscribers pay for eligible plans. Creator payments and content costs matter when interpreting the platform's economics. |
| Google Cloud | Google Cloud Platform rents computing, storage and data services. Google Workspace supplies productivity and collaboration subscriptions. |
| Android, Chrome and devices | Software and devices bring users to Google's services. The company also reports activities outside Google Services and Cloud under Other Bets. |
| Gemini | Gemini models and tools appear in consumer products and cloud services sold to developers and businesses. Product use can span more than one reporting category. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Google Cloud revenue reached USD 30.709 billion, up 103% from a year earlier. Google attributed the increase to growth in its cloud infrastructure and platform and sales of TPU systems to customers. [Quarterly report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000084/goog-20260930.htm) |

## Who buys it

Advertisers buy reach or measurable interactions, enterprises buy computing and applications, and consumers pay for devices, content or subscriptions.

No customer or group of affiliated customers exceeded 10% of revenue in 2023–2025. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000018/goog-20251231.htm)
