## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Oracle Cloud Infrastructure | OCI rents computing capacity, storage, networking and database services. Metered usage and contractual commitments are different measures. |
| Cloud applications | Fusion and NetSuite handle functions such as finance, human resources and supply chains. Customers access the software as a service. |
| Licenses and support | A software license gives a customer usage rights. Update rights and support contracts generate a different stream of revenue. |
| Hardware and services | Oracle also supplies engineered systems and hardware support, implementation, consulting and other services. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| FY2027 Q1 | Of USD 11.6 billion in cloud revenue, infrastructure contributed USD 7.4 billion and cloud applications USD 4.2 billion. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526387905/orcl-ex99_1.htm) |

## Who buys it

Corporate and public-sector customers may run applications on Oracle's infrastructure, license software on their own equipment, or buy support and consulting.

Oracle reported that no individual customer accounted for 10% or more of revenue in any of the fiscal years ended May 2026, 2025 or 2024. A contract announcement and the revenue recognized during one year measure different things. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526277521/orcl-20260531.htm)
