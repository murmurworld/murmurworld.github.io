## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Online and physical stores | Amazon recognizes retail revenue on merchandise it sells itself. Books, electronics, groceries and other goods reach customers through stores and delivery networks. |
| Third-party seller services | Independent merchants pay commissions or fulfillment and shipping fees. In the marketplace model the merchant's whole selling price generally does not become Amazon revenue. |
| AWS | A business can rent computing, storage and databases and pay for consumption or commit to capacity. AWS is also a separately reported operating segment. |
| Advertising and subscriptions | Advertisers buy placements, including ads alongside shopping results. Prime and other subscription offerings generate membership or subscription fees. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | AWS revenue reached USD 45.984 billion, up 39% from a year earlier. This segment collects fees from businesses that use cloud computing, storage and databases. [Quarterly report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000040/amzn-20260930.htm) |

## Who buys it

Shoppers, marketplace merchants, advertisers and cloud customers each purchase a different service from the same group.

When an independent seller sells through Amazon, the seller is the merchant of record. Amazon earns a commission and, when applicable, fulfillment and shipping fees. The gross merchandise purchase and Amazon's recognized seller-service revenue measure different things. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm)

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
