## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Advertising | Businesses buy placements across the apps. Ad impressions and the average price per ad help explain revenue changes. |
| Family of Apps | Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp provide social and messaging services. Paid messaging and related offerings complement advertising. |
| Meta AI | AI models and assistants are embedded across products. The underlying activity may contribute to engagement or advertising without being a separately reported revenue line. |
| Reality Labs | The business develops headsets, smart glasses and related platforms, software or content. Its costs and revenue are reported separately from Family of Apps. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |

## Who buys it

Advertisers are the principal paying customers of Family of Apps. The people viewing an ad and the advertiser paying for it are different participants.

No customer accounted for at least 10% of revenue in 2023–2025. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026003942/meta-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Meta sells access to audiences in its apps, while AppLovin supplies technology for matching ad demand with available inventory. Alphabet also sells ads in Search and YouTube.
