## Who buys it

Government agencies and commercial customers contract for software platforms, implementation and support. Deployment and continued use can be distinct contract stages.

In the first half of 2026, government customers generated 52% of revenue and commercial customers 48%. US customers accounted for 80%. No individual customer exceeded 10%. [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165526000041/pltr-20260630.htm)
