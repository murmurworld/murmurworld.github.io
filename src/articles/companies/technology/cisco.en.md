## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Networking | Switches, routers and wireless access equipment connect users and data centers. Internet and service-provider infrastructure is another paying market. |
| Security | Network and endpoint security, identity tools and related subscriptions protect systems and manage access. |
| Collaboration | Webex and related calling, messaging, conferencing and contact-center tools serve organizations. |
| Observability | Splunk and observability tools bring logs and other operational data together to help customers investigate performance or security issues. Support services accompany hardware and software. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |

## Who buys it

Enterprises, network operators and public agencies buy hardware and subscriptions directly and through channel partners.

One unnamed global channel partner represented approximately 11% of FY2026 revenue. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/858877/000085887726000132/csco-20260725.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Apple sells finished devices, Seagate sells drives for persistent storage and Cisco supplies equipment that moves data between machines. These are different points of demand, not a claim of a three-company contract.

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
