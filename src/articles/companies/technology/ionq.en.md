## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Quantum systems and services | Trapped-ion systems encode information in ions. Customers can access hardware, buy systems or fund research and development. Networking, sensing and related capabilities broaden the offering. |
| SkyWater foundry | A semiconductor foundry processes chip designs for customers. Its fabrication activity should be distinguished from sales of quantum computing access. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | The IonQ Platform segment recorded USD 90.3 million of revenue and SkyWater Foundry Services recorded USD 83.5 million. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1824920/000119312526449779/ionq-ex99_1.htm) |

## Who buys it

Research groups, government customers and companies contract for quantum systems, cloud access or technical services. Foundry customers purchase chip manufacturing.

IonQ reported one customer at 30% of third-quarter 2026 revenue. Over the first nine months, one customer exceeded the threshold at 16%. The filing does not name the customers in either period. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1824920/000119312526449829/ionq-20260930.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Cloud customers can rent computing and storage in metered units. IonQ also makes quantum computers accessible through cloud marketplaces.
