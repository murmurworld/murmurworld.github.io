## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Endpoint and cloud security | Falcon collects signals from endpoint devices and cloud workloads to help security teams detect, investigate and respond to threats. |
| Identity protection | Identity modules monitor accounts and access-related signals. Their buyer is typically the organization responsible for securing users and infrastructure. |
| Security information and event management | The platform brings security records together so analysts can search related events and investigate incidents. |
| Threat intelligence and managed services | Intelligence subscriptions and managed monitoring add research or human support. Professional services are a separate source of revenue from subscriptions. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |

## Who buys it

Organizations purchase subscriptions directly and through channel partners. Adding a module can expand revenue from an existing customer.

No channel partner or direct customer reached 10% of revenue during either the three or the six months ended July 31, 2026. [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1535527/000153552726000031/crwd-20260731.htm)

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
