## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Next-generation firewalls | Physical or virtual firewalls inspect and control network traffic. Subscriptions and support can be attached to the deployment. |
| Prisma Access and SASE | Cloud-delivered network access and security connect users to applications from multiple locations. |
| Cortex | Operations teams combine security telemetry, investigation and response workflows in a common platform. |
| Cloud and AI security | Cloud-security products help teams inspect and manage cloud workloads and applications. Product groupings and names can change as platforms are combined. |

## Who buys it

Enterprises and service providers buy hardware appliances, software and subscriptions through a mix of partners and direct sales.

Two distributors each accounted for at least 10% of FY2026 revenue and together contributed 30%. No end customer exceeded 10%. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1327567/000132756726000023/panw-20260731.htm)

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.
