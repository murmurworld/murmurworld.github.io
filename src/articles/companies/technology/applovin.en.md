## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Axon advertising platform | Advertisers select objectives and budgets. The platform helps choose placements and deliver ads against those goals. |
| MAX | Publishers can offer advertising inventory to competing sources of demand. This side of the business serves the owner of the app. |
| Adjust and Wurl | Adjust measures attribution and engagement. Wurl supplies connected-TV distribution and advertising technology, an additional use case beyond mobile inventory. |

## Who buys it

App developers sell advertising space and advertisers pay to reach users. Measurement tools help customers link an ad placement to an outcome.

No individual customer reached 10% of revenue in 2023–2025. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1751008/000175100826000010/app-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Meta sells access to audiences in its apps, while AppLovin supplies technology for matching ad demand with available inventory. Alphabet also sells ads in Search and YouTube.
