## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Data center hard drives | Nearline hard drives use rotating magnetic disks to store large quantities of data. Buyers care about capacity, reliability and the cost of storing each unit of data. |
| Mozaic and HAMR | Heat-assisted magnetic recording increases the amount of data that can be written on a platter. It is a recording technology used inside drives. |
| Edge and consumer storage | Seagate also sells drives and storage systems for other enterprise, edge and consumer applications. Their workloads and capacity requirements differ from nearline storage. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |

## Who buys it

Data center operators, equipment makers and distributors buy storage products. Capacity shipped in exabytes helps explain demand alongside the number of drives.

One unnamed customer represented about 14% of FY2026 consolidated revenue. By channel, OEMs contributed 81%, distributors 13% and retailers 6%. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1137789/000113778926000159/stx-20260703.htm)
