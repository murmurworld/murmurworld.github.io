## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Azure and server products | Companies rent computing, storage and databases or license server software for their own systems. Usage-based charges can grow even with a fixed number of customers. |
| Microsoft 365 | Businesses and consumers pay for productivity and collaboration software. The productivity business also includes LinkedIn and Dynamics business applications. |
| Windows and devices | PC makers license Windows for new computers. Microsoft also sells devices, search advertising and related products or services in its personal computing business. |
| Xbox and gaming | Microsoft sells consoles, games, subscriptions and content. Platform fees and sales of Microsoft's own games represent different activities. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| FY2026 Q4 | Azure and other cloud services revenue grew 43% year over year. [Company results or filing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q4/press-release-webcast) |

## Who buys it

Enterprises and governments buy cloud and software contracts, PC makers license Windows, and consumers buy subscriptions, devices and games.

No single customer accounted for more than 10% of revenue in fiscal 2024, 2025 or 2026. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Cloud customers can rent computing and storage in metered units. IonQ also makes quantum computers accessible through cloud marketplaces.

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
