Apple sells iPhones, Macs and accessories, alongside services such as device support, digital content and cloud storage. Consumers, businesses, schools and governments buy from Apple, mobile carriers and resellers. Hardware sales and paid services monetize different parts of the same installed base.

## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone | Apple supplies smartphones with its own operating system and sells them through its direct channel, carriers and retailers. |
| Mac and iPad | Mac computers and iPad tablets serve individuals, businesses and education customers. Each family is reported separately in the product sales note. |
| Wearables, Home and Accessories | Watches, headphones, home devices and accessories extend the installed base. Apple reports these items together rather than breaking out each model's sales. |
| Services | Digital content, advertising, cloud services, AppleCare and payment services generate fees through different arrangements. A recurring subscription and an App Store commission have different underlying transactions. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| FY2026 Q3 | Revenue grew 16% year over year to USD 109.4 billion. [Company results or filing](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/07/apple-reports-third-quarter-results/) |

## Who buys it

Consumers, businesses, schools and governments buy from Apple, mobile carriers and resellers. Hardware sales and paid services monetize different parts of the same installed base.

Indirect channels accounted for about 60% of FY2026 net sales. These include carriers and resellers. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Apple sells finished devices, Seagate sells drives for persistent storage and Cisco supplies equipment that moves data between machines. These are different points of demand, not a claim of a three-company contract.

Companies in this group · [Seagate](/en/blog/companies/technology/seagate/) · [Cisco](/en/blog/companies/technology/cisco/)

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
