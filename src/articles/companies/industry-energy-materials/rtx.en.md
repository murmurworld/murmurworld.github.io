## Who buys it

Airframers, airline operators, governments and contractors buy commercial aerospace systems, propulsion and defense products under supply or service contracts.

Sales to the U.S. government represented 35.5% of RTX net sales in the third quarter of 2026 and 36.4% over nine months. RTX separately reports foreign military sales made through the U.S. government and excludes them from those percentages. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101829/000010182926000031/rtx-20260930.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Engine and aircraft system suppliers serve airframers and airlines. SpaceX sells launch services and access to its satellite communications network.
