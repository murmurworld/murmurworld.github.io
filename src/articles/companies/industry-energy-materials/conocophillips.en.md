## Who buys it

The upstream group sells produced oil, natural gas, natural gas liquids and other volumes to refiners, utilities, traders and marketers.

ConocoPhillips’ Lower 48 segment sold approximately $5.3 billion to one unnamed pipeline company in 2025. This was about 10% of consolidated sales and other operating revenues. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1163165/000116316526000009/cop-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Upstream businesses sell oil and gas. Refineries turn crude into products, while LNG plants liquefy gas for shipment. The integrated companies operate across more of those stages than a producer concentrated upstream.
