## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Copper | Mined ore is processed into copper concentrate or other copper products for smelters and industrial supply chains. |
| Iron ore | Mines and transport infrastructure supply iron ore to steelmakers, principally through seaborne markets. |
| Steelmaking coal | Metallurgical coal is used in conventional blast-furnace steel production. It serves a different use from coal burned for electricity. |
| Potash and other activities | Jansen is a potash project in Canada. Project development spending should be distinguished from sales by producing assets. |

## Who buys it

Steel mills, smelters, refiners and traders buy ores or concentrates. Commodity sales and realized prices differ from profit attributable to BHP's project interests.

BHP assigns geographic revenue by customer location. In the year ended June 2026, customers located in China accounted for $34.206 billion of the group’s $58.760 billion of revenue. The geographic total groups many buyers together. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/811809/000119312526354647/bhp-20260630.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Iron ore, copper and gold are sold as different products. Even within copper, concentrate is an intermediate containing copper, while a cathode is refined metal.
