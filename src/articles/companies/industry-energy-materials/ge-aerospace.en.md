## Who buys it

Aircraft and engine manufacturers, airlines, leasing companies and government agencies buy engines, components and long-term service support.

The installed base includes about 50,000 commercial engines and 30,000 military engines. It includes engines on parked aircraft as well as those in service. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40545/000004054526000008/ge-20251231.htm)
