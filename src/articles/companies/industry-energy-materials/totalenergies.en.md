TotalEnergies produces oil and gas, sells LNG, refines fuels and has an integrated power business. Industrial users, utilities, trading partners and consumers purchase hydrocarbons, refined products and electricity.

## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |

## Who buys it

Industrial users, utilities, trading partners and consumers purchase hydrocarbons, refined products and electricity.

Sembcorp is a named customer for TotalEnergies LNG. The 2025 annual report describes a contract signed in 2024 to deliver 0.8 million tonnes annually for 16 years from 2027. Contracted volumes and a customer's share of sales measure different things. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://totalenergies.com/system/files/documents/totalenergies_form-20-f-2025_2026_en.pdf)

## Its place in the sector

Upstream businesses sell oil and gas. Refineries turn crude into products, while LNG plants liquefy gas for shipment. The integrated companies operate across more of those stages than a producer concentrated upstream.
