## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Gold | Gold-bearing ore is mined and processed. Doré is a partly refined metal product that goes on to a refinery. |
| Copper, silver and other metals | Certain mines also produce copper, silver, lead or zinc. Production, sales and the metals economically attributable to Newmont are separately defined. |

## Who buys it

Refiners, smelters and metal buyers take output in the form of doré or concentrate. Mine ownership and payable metal affect what the company recognizes.

Standard Chartered accounted for 27% ($6.009 billion) of Newmont’s 2025 sales and JPMorgan Chase for 11% ($2.499 billion). The filing identifies the banks as buyers in the sales note. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1164727/000116472726000010/nem-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Iron ore, copper and gold are sold as different products. Even within copper, concentrate is an intermediate containing copper, while a cathode is refined metal.
