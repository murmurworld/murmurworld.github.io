## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| On-site supply | A production plant on or near a major customer's site delivers gas through a pipeline, often under a long-duration agreement. |
| Merchant liquid | Liquefied industrial gases are delivered by truck to customer storage tanks. Local distribution economics matter. |
| Packaged gas | Cylinders serve users with smaller or more varied needs. Product purity and handling requirements vary by application. |
| Engineering | The engineering business designs and constructs gas-processing plants for outside customers as well as group operations. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Underlying sales grew 5%, with pricing adding 3% and volume 2% under the company’s presentation. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1707925/000165495426009199/lin_ex991.htm) |

## Who buys it

Steel mills, chemical and electronics plants, hospitals and smaller users buy gas through different delivery systems. Equipment customers can also buy complete plants.
