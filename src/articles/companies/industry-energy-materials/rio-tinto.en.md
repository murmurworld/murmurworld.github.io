## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Iron ore | Mining, railways and ports move iron ore into steel supply chains. |
| Copper | Copper mines produce concentrate and other copper products. The amount attributable to Rio Tinto depends on the project's ownership. |
| Aluminium | Bauxite mining, alumina refining and aluminium smelting are separate steps from ore to usable metal. |
| Lithium and other minerals | Lithium assets add materials used in rechargeable batteries. Other mineral operations and product-group boundaries should be read in the filing for the stated year. |

## Who buys it

Steel, aluminum and copper supply chains buy ores, refined metals or intermediate materials. The same group can both extract and process a material.

Steel mills use Rio Tinto's iron ore. Its 2025 report describes testing with universities and mills on lower-emission blast-furnace feed mixes. China Baowu separately owned 46% of the Western Range iron ore venture. That percentage describes mine ownership. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/863064/000162828026009531/rio-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Iron ore, copper and gold are sold as different products. Even within copper, concentrate is an intermediate containing copper, while a cathode is refined metal.
