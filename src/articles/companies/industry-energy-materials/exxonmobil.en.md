## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Upstream | Exploration and production bring oil and natural gas to market. Production volume and realized prices both affect sales. |
| Energy Products | Refineries turn crude into fuels and other products. A refinery's economics involve the difference between feedstock and output prices. |
| Chemical Products | Petrochemical operations supply polymers and intermediate chemicals to manufacturers. |
| Specialty Products | Lubricants, basestocks, specialty materials and related products are sold to industrial and consumer channels. |

## Its place in the sector

Upstream businesses sell oil and gas. Refineries turn crude into products, while LNG plants liquefy gas for shipment. The integrated companies operate across more of those stages than a producer concentrated upstream.
