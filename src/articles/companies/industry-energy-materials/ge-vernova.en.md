## Who buys it

Utilities, grid operators, industrial users and project developers buy capital equipment and contract for installation or maintenance.

GE Vernova sells gas turbines to utilities, independent power producers and industrial users. Grid equipment serves utilities and industrial customers moving power from generation to consumption. A power station and the network carrying its electricity can have different buyers.
