SpaceX sells launch services and operates Starlink, a satellite network through which customers purchase internet connectivity. Commercial and government customers book launch services, subscribers pay for connectivity, and software customers purchase digital services.

## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Falcon launch services carry customer payloads into space. The company also develops the Starship launch system. |
| Connectivity | Starlink sells satellite broadband subscriptions and user terminals. Selling equipment and maintaining an active subscriber are different revenue events. |

## Who buys it

Commercial and government customers book launch services, subscribers pay for connectivity, and software customers purchase digital services.

In the third quarter of 2026, customer A accounted for 16.6% and customer B for 34.5% of SpaceX’s consolidated revenue. Customer A generated business across all three segments. Customer B's revenue was in SI. The filing does not identify either customer.
