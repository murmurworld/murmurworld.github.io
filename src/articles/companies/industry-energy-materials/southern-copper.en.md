## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Copper | Mining, concentrating, smelting and refining can yield products at several stages, including concentrates and refined copper. |
| By-products | Molybdenum, zinc, silver and other metals are recovered alongside copper. Their revenue should not be treated as additional copper tonnage. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Copper production was 232,396 tonnes. Cash cost net of by-product credits was USD 0.09 per pound. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001838/000110465926117588/scco-ex99d1.htm) |

## Who buys it

Smelters, refiners, industrial metal users and traders buy copper and by-products from the group's operations.

Southern Copper's largest customer represented 8.7% of 2025 sales, and the five largest together represented 24.2%. The 19.0% figure in the same note measures the largest year-end trade receivable, a different denominator. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001838/000110465926021492/scco-20251231x10k.htm)
