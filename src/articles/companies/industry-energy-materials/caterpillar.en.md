## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Construction equipment | Excavators, loaders and related machines are bought for earthmoving, building and infrastructure projects. |
| Resource industries | Large haul trucks and other mining equipment are used in extracting and moving material. |
| Energy and transportation | Engines, turbines, generator sets and rail products supply energy, industrial and transport customers. |
| Financial products and services | Dealer and customer financing, parts and aftermarket support extend the relationship beyond delivery of a machine. |

## Who buys it

Independent dealers supply contractors, mines, transport operators and energy customers. Financial services can help dealers and end users purchase machines.
