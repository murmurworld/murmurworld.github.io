## Who buys it

The upstream business sells oil and gas, while downstream operations supply fuel, lubricants and related products to industrial and retail channels.

In 2025 Chevron supplied about 8,600 Chevron- and Texaco-branded U.S. stations through its retail and marketing network. Around 380 locations were company-owned or leased. Many different operators can therefore buy fuel under the same roadside brand. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93410/000009341026000078/cvx-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Upstream businesses sell oil and gas. Refineries turn crude into products, while LNG plants liquefy gas for shipment. The integrated companies operate across more of those stages than a producer concentrated upstream.
