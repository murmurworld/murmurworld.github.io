## Who buys it

Utilities, industrial users, traders, fuel distributors and motorists buy gas, electricity, chemicals and fuels at different points in the supply chain.

Shell can supply LNG it produces and LNG it buys. A ten-year offtake agreement effective in January 2025 lets it purchase up to 1.6 million tonnes a year from Oman LNG. Oman LNG is the supplier in this transaction. Shell combines purchased and produced cargoes in the portfolio it delivers to customers. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1306965/000162828026017024/shel-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Upstream businesses sell oil and gas. Refineries turn crude into products, while LNG plants liquefy gas for shipment. The integrated companies operate across more of those stages than a producer concentrated upstream.
