A cable factory uses copper, while a steelworks uses iron ore. What a mining company sells depends on the customer and how far the material has been processed. The copper concentrate sold by Freeport-McMoRan and BHP offers a way to see how mined rock becomes material for a power cable.

## Smelters buy concentrate. Fabricators buy refined copper.

At a mine, the ore is crushed and its copper-bearing minerals are concentrated to make **copper concentrate**. In South Korea, LS MnM smelts and refines concentrate into high-purity copper cathodes. Fabricators can buy cathodes to make wire rod for cables or sheet for electronic components. The sequence is **ore → concentrate → cathodes → rod and sheet**. [LS MnM copper smelting operations, Korean](https://lsmnm.com/business/business_overView) · [Poongsan’s fabricated products, Korean](https://join.poongsan.co.kr/introduce/business.aspx) · [USGS, uses of copper](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/copper-statistics-and-information)

Freeport-McMoRan sends some concentrate from its Morenci mine in the United States to affiliated smelters and sells other volumes to external buyers. Escondida, a Chilean mine in which BHP holds a stake, also produces concentrate and refined copper. A smelter considering concentrate from both companies can compare copper content, impurities that affect processing, contract terms for treatment and refining charges, and shipping distance. Equal weights of concentrate can yield different amounts of recoverable copper and cost different amounts to process. [Freeport-McMoRan’s Morenci technical report](https://www.fcx.com/sites/fcx/files/documents/operations/TRS-morenci.pdf) · [BHP 2026 annual report](https://www.bhp.com/-/media/documents/investors/annual-reports/2026/260818_bhpannualreport2026.pdf) · [Freeport-McMoRan 2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/831259/000083125926000012/fcx-20251231.htm)

For example, 100 dry tonnes of concentrate at 25% copper contain 25 tonnes of the metal. The same mass at 30% contains 30 tonnes. For an actual purchase, the smelter checks how much it can recover, how much the contract treats as payable, and what treatment and refining cost. [KGHM’s copper market explanation](https://kghm.com/en/about-us/our-industry/copper-market)

## Compare iron ore in the same form

Steelworks buy iron ore to make iron and steel. Rio Tinto’s Pilbara Blend Fines are suitable for sintering, a step that joins small particles into a feed material. BHP’s Western Australia Iron Ore business also produces fines, including Newman High-grade Fines. A plant buying fine ore can compare these products for the same job. Buyers check iron content, impurities, delivery terms, and whether the ore fits the mix of feedstock the plant uses. [Rio Tinto iron ore products](https://www.riotinto.com/products/iron-ore) · [BHP Western Australia Iron Ore technical report in its 2025 Form 20-F](https://www.bhp.com/-/media/documents/investors/annual-reports/2025/bhpform20f2025.pdf)

**Pellets** are iron ore formed into small balls before delivery. To compare them with fines bought for sintering, check what further processing the steelworks would need and how each fits its blast furnace feed mix. [Geoscience Australia on iron ore](https://www.ga.gov.au/education/minerals-energy/australian-mineral-facts/iron)

## Read mine output and company sales separately

- **Freeport-McMoRan’s 2025 10-K** reports sales volumes and realized prices. A provisional sales price can be adjusted later. When reading unit net cash costs, check whether income from by-products such as gold or molybdenum was deducted from costs. [Freeport-McMoRan 2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/831259/000083125926000012/fcx-20251231.htm)
- **BHP’s 2026 annual report** separately discusses Copper and Western Australia Iron Ore for the year ended June 30, 2026. BHP lists a 57.5% ownership interest in Escondida. Read the production table’s footnotes to see whether it counts the full mine’s output or a company’s share. Antofagasta’s production KPI shows how one total can use different bases for different mines. [BHP 2026 annual report](https://www.bhp.com/-/media/documents/investors/annual-reports/2026/260818_bhpannualreport2026.pdf#page=12) · [Antofagasta 2025 production KPI](https://www.antofagasta.co.uk/investors/annual-report-2025/kpis/)
- **Rio Tinto’s 2025 annual report** separates production from shipments for Pilbara iron ore. Some ore produced in a given period can be shipped later. The output figure alone does not establish sales. [Rio Tinto 2025 annual report](https://www.riotinto.com/en/invest/reports/annual-report)
