## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Data center SSDs | Flash, a controller and firmware are combined into a drive designed for sustained enterprise workloads. |
| Embedded storage | OEMs integrate flash into phones, PCs and other devices. The drive or embedded package must fit the host device's power and space limits. |
| Consumer storage | Memory cards, USB flash drives and portable SSDs give users removable storage for cameras, computers and other equipment. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| FY2027 Q1 | Data-center revenue grew 61% from the previous quarter to USD 4.80 billion. Consumer revenue declined 29% over the same interval. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2023554/000162828026068243/sndkq1-27ex991xpressrelease.htm) |

## Who buys it

Enterprise storage buyers, device makers and retail customers buy products with different controllers, form factors and support requirements.

Two unnamed customers each accounted for 12% of net revenue in the quarter ended October 2, 2026. [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2023554/000162828026068601/sndk-20261002.htm)

## Its place in the sector

TSMC manufactures customer designs. Samsung, SK hynix and Micron make memory, while Sandisk obtains flash wafers through ventures with Kioxia. Intel and Samsung also have foundry businesses. Texas Instruments manufactures most products in-house, while Analog Devices mixes internal and outsourced production.

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

The October filing cover predates the 8-for-1 share split in November.
