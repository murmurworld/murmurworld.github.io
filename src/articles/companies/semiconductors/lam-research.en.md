## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Etch | Etch equipment selectively removes material from a wafer. High-aspect-ratio features are particularly important in vertically stacked memory. |
| Deposition | Tools add tightly controlled layers during chip fabrication, including films deposited on the walls of narrow features. |
| Clean | Wet-processing and cleaning tools remove particles and residues between manufacturing steps. |
| Installed-base support | Customer support includes spares, service and upgrades. This activity follows the size and utilization of the installed fleet as well as new fab projects. |

## Who buys it

Memory and logic manufacturers buy tools for their fabs and purchase support for systems already installed.

Four customers accounted for approximately 16%, 15%, 12% and 12% of FY2026 revenue. The disclosure does not identify them. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/707549/000070754926000037/lrcx-20260628.htm)

## Its place in the sector

ASML prints circuit patterns with light. Applied Materials and Lam add or remove material. KLA looks for defects and measures dimensions while a wafer is still in production.
