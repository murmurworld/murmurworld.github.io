## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Advanced process foundry | A fabrication process determines which transistor and interconnect features a designer can use. Customers order wafer production rather than purchasing a TSMC-branded processor. |
| High performance computing | The platform category covers foundry production for data center processors, accelerators, network and other high-performance devices. |
| Smartphone and other end markets | Application processors and supporting chips go into handsets. TSMC also reports platform categories for IoT, automotive, consumer electronics and other uses. |
| Advanced packaging | CoWoS brings a processor and high-bandwidth memory together in one package. Chip-on-wafer technologies address the connections between separately manufactured dies. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Nodes at 7 nm and below accounted for 79% of wafer revenue. The 3 nm node contributed 33%, and 2 nm contributed 8%. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000104617926000726/a3q26e_withguidancexfinal.htm) |

## Who buys it

Chip design companies and system makers supply their designs. TSMC manufactures wafers for those customers and offers packaging services.

In 2025, customer A accounted for 19% of net revenue and customer B for 17%. The disclosure identifies them by letter. [20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828026025362/tsm-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

TSMC manufactures customer designs. Samsung, SK hynix and Micron make memory, while Sandisk obtains flash wafers through ventures with Kioxia. Intel and Samsung also have foundry businesses. Texas Instruments manufactures most products in-house, while Analog Devices mixes internal and outsourced production.

## Where to look in the filing

Item 4 of Form 20-F describes the business and operating footprint. Item 5 discusses operating and financial results. The consolidated notes distinguish geographical and product disclosures. Interim releases furnished on Form 6-K specify the period each table covers.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
