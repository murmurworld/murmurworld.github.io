## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Custom AI silicon | Broadcom helps data center customers design and implement accelerators for their own computing workloads instead of buying a general-purpose GPU alone. |
| Ethernet and network silicon | Switch and routing chips direct packets across networks. Connectivity and storage components also connect servers to network or storage infrastructure. |
| Wireless and broadband components | Radio-frequency filters and connectivity chips serve handset and networking equipment makers. Broadband and industrial semiconductor products address additional end markets. |
| Infrastructure software | VMware virtualization software lets customers share physical servers across workloads. Mainframe, security and related software generally generates license, subscription or support revenue. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |

## Who buys it

Hyperscalers commission silicon, networking and wireless equipment makers buy chips, and enterprise IT departments license software.

One semiconductor distributor represented 32% of consolidated net revenue in FY2025. A distributor pools orders from end customers whose individual contributions may be much smaller. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016825000121/avgo-20251102.htm)

## Its place in the sector

NVIDIA, AMD and Qualcomm outsource wafer production. Broadcom relies principally on external foundries but also operates some manufacturing facilities.

Companies in this group · [NVIDIA](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/nvidia/) · [AMD](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/amd/) · [Qualcomm](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/qualcomm/)

Buyer Broadcom · Supplier TSMC. About 95% of wafers produced by its contract manufacturers in FY2025 came from TSMC. [Broadcom FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016825000121/avgo-20251102.htm)

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
