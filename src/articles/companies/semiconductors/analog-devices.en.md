  question: Why put an Analog Devices chip between a temperature sensor and a computer?

Analog Devices makes chips that convert physical measurements to digital information, and devices that manage power in factories, vehicles and communications equipment. Designers of factories, vehicles, network equipment and instruments buy components through direct and distributor channels.

## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Data converters and amplifiers | Converters link digital processors to physical measurements. Amplifiers condition small signals before conversion or drive outputs after conversion. |
| Power management | Regulators, controllers and battery-management components supply stable power and monitor batteries in vehicles and other equipment. |
| Radio frequency and mixed signal | RF chips process high-frequency signals in communications and instrumentation. DSPs and mixed-signal systems combine signal processing and control. |

## Who buys it

Designers of factories, vehicles, network equipment and instruments buy components through direct and distributor channels.

Third-party distributors handled about 56% of sales in the year ended November 1, 2025. Distributors 1 and 3 represented 24% and 13% of total revenue. The table does not name them. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6281/000000628125000153/adi-20251101.htm)

## Its place in the sector

TSMC manufactures customer designs. Samsung, SK hynix and Micron make memory, while Sandisk obtains flash wafers through ventures with Kioxia. Intel and Samsung also have foundry businesses. Texas Instruments manufactures most products in-house, while Analog Devices mixes internal and outsourced production.

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
