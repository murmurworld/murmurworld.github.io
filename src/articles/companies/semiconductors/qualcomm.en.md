## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Handset chips | Snapdragon platforms combine application processing, graphics, modem and connectivity functions for smartphone makers. |
| Automotive platforms | Vehicle makers and their suppliers use connectivity, digital cockpit and driver-assistance computing platforms. |
| Internet of Things | Processors and wireless components connect industrial devices, consumer electronics and edge computers. |
| Patent licensing | QTL licenses cellular intellectual property. The licensee pays under its agreement even though licensing and chip sales are separate transactions. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| FY2026 | Within QCT, handset revenue fell 13% year over year, while automotive grew 52% and IoT grew 8%. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000080432826000127/qcom092726erex991.htm) |

## Who buys it

Device makers buy chips through the semiconductor business and license cellular technology through a separate business.

Customer/licensee x generated 22% of FY2026 revenue and y generated 20%. Qualcomm uses letters for the counterparties. A handset producer can buy chips and also pay licensing fees. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000080432826000128/qcom-20260927.htm)

## Its place in the sector

NVIDIA, AMD and Qualcomm outsource wafer production. Broadcom relies principally on external foundries but also operates some manufacturing facilities.

Companies in this group · [NVIDIA](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/nvidia/) · [AMD](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/amd/) · [Broadcom](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/broadcom/)

Buyer Qualcomm · Supplier TSMC, Samsung and GlobalFoundries. Primary foundry suppliers for wafer fabrication. [Qualcomm FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000080432826000128/qcom-20260927.htm)
