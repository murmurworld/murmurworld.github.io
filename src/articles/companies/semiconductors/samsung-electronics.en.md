Samsung Electronics manufactures DRAM and NAND, offers foundry services and sells finished products including phones, TVs and appliances. Equipment distributors and consumers buy finished devices. Device companies also buy memory, displays or outsourced chip production.

## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Memory | DRAM supplies working memory, NAND stores files without power, and HBM stacks DRAM close to the processor to move large amounts of data. |
| System LSI and foundry | System LSI designs mobile processors, image sensors and other chips. Foundry manufactures chips designed by customers. |
| Devices and appliances | The DX division sells mobile devices, network equipment, televisions, monitors and home appliances through retail and business channels. |
| Displays and Harman | Samsung Display supplies panels to device makers. Harman sells automotive electronics, connected systems and consumer or professional audio products. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Device Solutions represented 71.2% of consolidated revenue for January–September, compared with 39.0% for full-year 2025. [Company results or filing](https://dart.fss.or.kr/dsaf001/main.do?rcpNo=20261116001482) |

## Who buys it

Equipment distributors and consumers buy finished devices. Device companies also buy memory, displays or outsourced chip production.

## Its place in the sector

TSMC manufactures customer designs. Samsung, SK hynix and Micron make memory, while Sandisk obtains flash wafers through ventures with Kioxia. Intel and Samsung also have foundry businesses. Texas Instruments manufactures most products in-house, while Analog Devices mixes internal and outsourced production.

## Where to look in the filing

In the DART business report, Business Overview sets out principal products, services and sales. The consolidated notes cover segments and major customers. Interim income statements distinguish the three-month column from year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
