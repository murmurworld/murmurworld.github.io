## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| EUV lithography | Extreme-ultraviolet light projects very fine circuit patterns onto a wafer. Different process layers require different exposures. |
| DUV lithography | Deep-ultraviolet tools print many other layers and serve both advanced and mature processes. One chip may use DUV and EUV steps. |
| Installed systems and process control | Service, spares, upgrades, metrology, inspection and computational lithography help customers maintain and tune equipment already in the fab. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Installed Base Management generated EUR 3.29 billion from services and upgrades for customers’ installed machines. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026065813/pressreleasefinancialresul.htm) |

## Who buys it

Chipmakers buy lithography systems to equip fabrication lines, then pay for parts, servicing or upgrades over the installed fleet's life.

ASML’s largest customer generated 23.9% of 2025 net sales. The two largest together accounted for 38.0%. The disclosure does not identify them. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/71076aaad607de4d/original/asml-2025-annual-report-based-on-us-gaap.pdf)

## Its place in the sector

ASML prints circuit patterns with light. Applied Materials and Lam add or remove material. KLA looks for defects and measures dimensions while a wafer is still in production.
