## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Wafer and reticle inspection | Inspection systems scan patterned or bare wafers and masks so a fab can locate defects before processing more material. |
| Metrology and process control | Metrology measures features such as dimensions or alignment. Specialty semiconductor processes, printed circuit boards and component inspection are additional activities. |
| Services | Service contracts, replacement parts and upgrades support installed inspection and measurement instruments. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |

## Who buys it

Semiconductor and electronics manufacturers buy tools to detect defects, measure features and manage yields.

KLA identifies TSMC in its table of customers exceeding 10% of FY2026 revenue, primarily from Semiconductor Process Control. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/319201/000031920126000027/klac-20260630.htm)

## Its place in the sector

ASML prints circuit patterns with light. Applied Materials and Lam add or remove material. KLA looks for defects and measures dimensions while a wafer is still in production.

Companies in this group · [ASML](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/asml/) · [Applied Materials](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/applied-materials/) · [Lam Research](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/lam-research/)

Buyer TSMC · Supplier KLA. Process-control supplier KLA identifies TSMC as a customer above its 10% threshold. [KLA FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/319201/000031920126000027/klac-20260630.htm)
