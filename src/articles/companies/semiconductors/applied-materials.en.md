Applied Materials makes and services equipment that deposits, removes, modifies and inspects layers on a wafer as chips are fabricated. Chip and display makers order capital equipment. Service customers purchase parts, maintenance and upgrades for their installed tools.

## Who buys it

Chip and display makers order capital equipment. Service customers purchase parts, maintenance and upgrades for their installed tools.

Two unnamed customers contributed approximately 20% and 14% of revenue for the nine months ended July 26, 2026. No other individual customer exceeded 10%. [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6951/000162828026058235/amat-20260726.htm)

## Its place in the sector

ASML prints circuit patterns with light. Applied Materials and Lam add or remove material. KLA looks for defects and measures dimensions while a wafer is still in production.

Companies in this group · [ASML](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/asml/) · [Lam Research](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/lam-research/) · [KLA](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/kla/)

Deposition and etch equipment · Applied Materials and Lam Research sell tools for adding and removing thin films..
