## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| EPYC server CPUs | A server's general-purpose processor runs operating systems, databases and other workloads. EPYC processors can share a rack with dedicated AI accelerators. |
| Instinct accelerators | Customers install these processors in servers to train models or run inference. ROCm software connects developers' tools to the hardware. |
| Ryzen PC processors | Notebook and desktop makers buy these processors to run ordinary applications, with integrated graphics or AI engines on selected models. |
| Gaming chips | Radeon GPUs render images. Semi-custom system-on-chips combine processing functions for console manufacturers. |
| Embedded and adaptive computing | FPGAs can be reconfigured after manufacture. Engineers use them and embedded processors in communications, industrial control and other specialized equipment. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Data Center generated USD 8.5 billion, or 64% of revenue. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000188/q32026991.htm) |

## Who buys it

Cloud and enterprise servers, PC makers, console manufacturers and design engineers buy processors for different workloads.

No single customer reached 10% of consolidated net revenue in FY2024 or FY2025. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000018/amd-20251227.htm)

## Its place in the sector

NVIDIA, AMD and Qualcomm outsource wafer production. Broadcom relies principally on external foundries but also operates some manufacturing facilities.

Companies in this group · [NVIDIA](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/nvidia/) · [Broadcom](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/broadcom/) · [Qualcomm](/en/blog/companies/semiconductors/qualcomm/)

Buyer AMD · Supplier TSMC and other foundries. Wafer fabrication. The filing also identifies GlobalFoundries, UMC and Samsung for parts of its manufacturing footprint. [AMD FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000018/amd-20251227.htm)

Data-center accelerators · NVIDIA, AMD and Intel. NVIDIA also names cloud operators designing their own chips.. [NVIDIA FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
