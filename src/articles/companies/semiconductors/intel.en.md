## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Client processors | Core and Core Ultra processors run notebook and desktop applications. Intel supplies these components to PC manufacturers and the distribution channel. |
| Server CPUs and accelerators | Xeon processors run data center workloads. Specialized accelerators handle selected computational tasks alongside the host processor. |
| Network and custom silicon | Network interfaces and related silicon connect computing and communications equipment. Design wins depend on the customer's system architecture. |
| Intel Foundry | Wafer fabrication, packaging and process services let customers turn a chip design into physical devices. Intersegment sales to Intel products must be reconciled when reading consolidated revenue. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Data Center and AI generated USD 7.3 billion and the Client Computing and Physical AI Group (CCPG, formerly CCG) generated USD 8.1 billion. [Company results or filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086326000191/intc-20260926.htm) |

A Xeon 6 package connects compute dies made on Intel 3 with I/O dies made on Intel 7. For some other products Intel turns to TSMC to fabricate individual dies. In the third quarter of 2026, Intel Foundry reported $6.7 billion in revenue, of which $411 million came from external customers. [Intel annual filing on manufacturing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086326000011/intc-20251227.htm) · [Quarterly filing on foundry revenue](https://www.intc.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0000050863-26-000191/intc-20260926.htm)

## Who buys it

Computer manufacturers buy processors, cloud operators buy server platforms, and foundry customers contract for fabrication or packaging.

The same Xeon generation can reach a customer in two different ways. Lenovo’s ThinkSystem SR650 V4 accepts up to two Xeon 6 processors and lets the buyer configure memory and storage. AWS runs EC2 M8i instances on a custom Xeon 6. A company can buy the Lenovo server for its own racks or rent computing capacity from AWS. [Lenovo system specifications](https://lenovopress.lenovo.com/lp2127-thinksystem-sr650-v4-server) · [AWS M8i announcement](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2025/08/amazon-ec2-m8i-and-m8i-flex-instances-generally-available/)

The three largest customers accounted for 19%, 12% and 12% of 2025 consolidated net revenue. The filing identifies them as A, B and C. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086326000011/intc-20251227.htm)

## Its place in the sector

TSMC manufactures customer designs. Samsung, SK hynix and Micron make memory, while Sandisk obtains flash wafers through ventures with Kioxia. Intel and Samsung also have foundry businesses. Texas Instruments manufactures most products in-house, while Analog Devices mixes internal and outsourced production.
