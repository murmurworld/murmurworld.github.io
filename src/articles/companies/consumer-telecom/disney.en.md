## Who buys it

Distributors license content, advertisers buy audiences, subscribers pay for streaming and guests buy park visits, voyages and merchandise.

The same household can reach Disney programming through two payment routes. A direct streaming subscriber pays a subscription. A cable or satellite distributor pays Disney for the right to deliver programming to its own customers. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448926000066/dis-20261003.htm)
