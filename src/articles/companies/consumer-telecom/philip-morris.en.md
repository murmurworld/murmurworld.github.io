Philip Morris International sells cigarettes and smoke-free products such as heated-tobacco devices and consumables and oral nicotine products. Distributors and retailers sell tobacco and nicotine products to adult consumers in markets where permitted. Devices and repeat consumables have different shipment measures.

## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Combustible cigarettes | Cigarettes are sold under international and local brands. Manufacturer revenue and excise taxes should be distinguished when reading the financial statements. |
| Heated tobacco | IQOS devices use tobacco consumables. Device placements, shipments of units and users describe different aspects of the business. |
| Nicotine pouches and other smoke-free products | ZYN pouches are an oral nicotine product. The portfolio also includes other smoke-free categories. Volume measures differ by product form. |

## Who buys it

Distributors and retailers sell tobacco and nicotine products to adult consumers in markets where permitted. Devices and repeat consumables have different shipment measures.

One distributor in Europe generated 12% of Philip Morris International’s 2025 consolidated net revenues. A second distributor in East Asia, Australia and global travel retail generated 10%. The note identifies their segments but leaves them unnamed. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1413329/000162828026005939/pm-20251231.htm)
