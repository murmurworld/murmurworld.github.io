## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Streaming memberships | Members pay for access to a catalog of licensed and produced film and television content across devices. |
| Advertising-supported plans | A lower-priced plan can generate membership and advertising revenue. Audience measurement and delivery matter to advertisers. |
| Live programming and games | Live events and games broaden the offering. Cash paid to produce or license content and amortization recognized in earnings occur on different schedules. |

## Who buys it

Households pay for subscriptions. Advertisers also purchase access to audiences on eligible plans.

Netflix earns most of its revenue from monthly membership fees. It also sells advertising, adding advertisers as a second paying customer group. Hours watched measure viewing, while the monthly subscription is what the member is billed. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528026000034/nflx-20251231.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Netflix and Disney sell subscriptions and advertising on ad-supported services. Disney also earns revenue from businesses such as theme parks.

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
