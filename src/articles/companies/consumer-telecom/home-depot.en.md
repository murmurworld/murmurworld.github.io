| --- | --- |

| Installation and rentals | Customers can arrange installation or rent tools and equipment. Services help complete projects beyond the sale of individual products. |

## Who buys it

Do-it-yourself shoppers, customers arranging installations and professional contractors buy through stores, distribution networks and online channels.

No customer exceeded 10% of revenue in any of the three fiscal years covered by the annual report. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354950/000162828026019436/hd-20260201.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Walmart and Costco purchase goods for resale. Home Depot specializes in home improvement and serves tradespeople as well as households. Alibaba operates marketplaces that collect merchant service fees.

## Where to look in the filing

Item 1 of Form 10-K describes the business and its customers. Item 7 explains changes in results. Item 8 contains the consolidated statements and segment notes. In Form 10-Q, the starting and ending dates distinguish a quarter from fiscal year to date.

A subtotal can include the product lines beneath it. Segment, product and geographic views may describe the same underlying sales.
