## Who buys it

Merchants buy marketplace services and advertising, shoppers buy directly sold merchandise, and enterprises pay for cloud services.

On Taobao and Tmall, the shopper buying merchandise and the merchant paying Alibaba can be different parties. Merchants can bid for keywords and pay for clicks or impressions. Certain services charge commission on completed transactions. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000119312526231755/baba-20260331.htm)

## Its place in the sector

Walmart and Costco purchase goods for resale. Home Depot specializes in home improvement and serves tradespeople as well as households. Alibaba operates marketplaces that collect merchant service fees.
