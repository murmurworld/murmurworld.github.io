## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Wireless service | Postpaid, prepaid and business wireless services earn recurring fees from connections to the network. |
| Broadband and wireline | Fiber, fixed wireless and enterprise network offerings connect premises and customers. Availability depends on network coverage and technology. |
| Wireless equipment | Handsets and other devices generate sales distinct from service plans. Promotions and financing affect timing and presentation. |

## Who buys it

Consumer and business customers buy wireless and wireline services directly or through sales partners. It also supplies wholesale connectivity.

Verizon explicitly reported that no single customer accounted for more than 10% of operating revenue in either the third quarter or first nine months of 2026. The test measures the customer's share of revenue. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/732712/000073271226000052/vz-20260930.htm)
