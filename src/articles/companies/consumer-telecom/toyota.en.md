## Who buys it

Dealers and distributors deliver vehicles to consumers and fleets. Finance subsidiaries lend or lease to customers and support dealer inventories.

At March 2026, Toyota sold outside Japan through approximately 168 distributors in about 204 countries and regions. These distributors maintain local dealer networks. Reported vehicle unit sales include deliveries to unconsolidated distributors and dealers. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1094517/000119312526264811/d101983d20f.htm)
