## Who buys it

The group sells branded everyday goods through retailers, distributors and e-commerce. Consumers use the products while retailers often place the orders.

Walmart and its affiliates accounted for approximately 16% of P&G’s total sales in the fiscal year ended June 2026. This identifies the retailer buying the goods from P&G, one step before shoppers take them off the shelf. [Customer and distribution disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/80424/000008042426000103/pg-20260630.htm)
