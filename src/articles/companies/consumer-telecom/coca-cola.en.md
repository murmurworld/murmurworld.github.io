## Who buys it

Bottlers, distributors and retail or food-service channels connect the company to consumers. Selling concentrate and selling a finished bottle generate different sale values.

A single bottler accounted for 10% of Coca-Cola’s 2025 net operating revenues across its EMEA and Asia Pacific segments. The company did not name the bottler. A bottler buys concentrate and makes and distributes finished drinks. ## Its place in the sector

Household products, beverage concentrates, finished drinks, snacks and nicotine products use different routes to market. An independent bottler may sit between a beverage brand and a retailer.
