## What it sells

| Product or service | Use and users |
| --- | --- |
| Electric vehicles | Vehicle sales and leases supply passenger and commercial electric vehicles. Deliveries are a volume measure distinct from revenue. |
| Energy storage and generation | Powerwall and Megapack store electricity for homes and larger projects. Solar generation products address another part of the energy business. |
| Driver assistance and mobility services | Driver-assistance software and mobility initiatives are separate from the physical vehicle. Availability and recognized revenue depend on the product, market and applicable contract. |
| Credits and services | Regulatory credits are sold separately from vehicles. Charging, parts, repairs, used vehicles and other activities contribute to services and other revenue. |

| Reporting period | Company operating update |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 Q3 | Tesla produced 464,391 vehicles, delivered 486,532 and deployed 13.7 GWh of energy storage. [Company results or filing](https://ir.tesla.com/press-release/tesla-third-quarter-2026-production-deliveries-and-deployments) |

## Who buys it

Individuals, commercial fleets, homeowners and utilities buy or lease vehicles, energy equipment and related services.

Tesla reported that one customer represented over 10% of accounts receivable at September 30, 2026, all of it current. This captures unpaid invoices on a specific date, not that customer's share of sales over the year.
