<h2>Tracing a bottle through the checkout</h2>

<p>A shopper pays the retailer for the finished bottle. The retailer owes its supplier the wholesale price. If an independent bottler made the drink, the brand may have sold concentrate to that bottler rather than selling the final bottle to the store.</p>

<p><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/pepsico/">PepsiCo</a> includes concentrate sold to independent bottlers for finished products that reached Walmart when explaining its exposure to Walmart. Customer rebates or display allowances may reduce reported net revenue. Shelf price times bottles sold therefore does not reconstruct the manufacturer’s reported sales.</p>

<h2>The 14 companies in this sector</h2>

<div class="roster">

<div><h3>Branded consumer goods</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/procter-gamble/">Procter &amp; Gamble</a><small>PG</small></b><span>Sells branded cleaning, grooming, hygiene and personal care products through retailers and other distribution channels.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/coca-cola/">The Coca-Cola Company</a><small>KO</small></b><span>Supplies beverage concentrates and syrups to bottlers and also sells finished beverages in parts of its network.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/pepsico/">PepsiCo</a><small>PEP</small></b><span>Sells packaged snacks and beverages through direct distribution, retailers and bottling arrangements.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/philip-morris/">Philip Morris International</a><small>PM</small></b><span>Sells cigarettes and smoke-free products such as heated-tobacco devices and consumables and oral nicotine products.</span></li>

</ul></div>

<div><h3>Retailers and marketplaces</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/walmart/">Walmart</a><small>WMT</small></b><span>Buys and resells merchandise through stores, clubs and online channels, with additional income from membership and advertising services.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/costco/">Costco Wholesale</a><small>COST</small></b><span>Sells goods through membership warehouses. Merchandise sales and membership fees are reported separately.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/home-depot/">The Home Depot</a><small>HD</small></b><span>Sells building materials, home improvement goods and related services to households and professional contractors.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/alibaba/">Alibaba Group</a><small>BABA</small></b><span>Runs marketplaces that connect merchants with buyers and sells merchant services. Its operations also include cloud computing.</span></li>

</ul></div>

<div><h3>Vehicle manufacturers</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/tesla/">Tesla</a><small>TSLA</small></b><span>Sells electric vehicles, energy storage systems and related services. Vehicle deliveries and energy deployments measure different activities.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/toyota/">Toyota Motor</a><small>TM</small></b><span>Makes and sells vehicles and components. Its financial services operations finance vehicles for dealers and customers.</span></li>

</ul></div>

<div><h3>Content and streaming</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/netflix/">Netflix</a><small>NFLX</small></b><span>Earns revenue from streaming memberships and advertising. Content licensing or production precedes distribution to viewers.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/disney/">The Walt Disney Company</a><small>DIS</small></b><span>Produces and distributes filmed entertainment, runs streaming services and licenses brands. Its theme parks are a separate business.</span></li>

</ul></div>

<div><h3>Network operators</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/t-mobile/">T-Mobile US</a><small>TMUS</small></b><span>Sells wireless voice and data services and fixed wireless broadband, as well as devices used on its network.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/consumer-telecom/verizon/">Verizon</a><small>VZ</small></b><span>Sells wireless and fixed broadband connectivity to consumers and businesses, with equipment sales alongside service revenue.</span></li>

</ul></div>
