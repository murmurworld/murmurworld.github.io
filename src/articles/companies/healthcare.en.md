<h2>How a box of Humira becomes revenue</h2>

<p>AbbVie names three wholesalers through which substantially all its US pharmaceutical sales pass. A wholesaler supplies a pharmacy, which dispenses the prescription and submits a claim. A drug’s list price, patient out-of-pocket payment and the manufacturer’s net sales after rebates and discounts are different figures.</p>

<p>Start with the regulatory label for the ingredient and approved uses, then check patents and market exclusivity separately for the relevant country. Amjevita, a US biosimilar to Humira, was first approved in 2016. Approval and commercial launch are separate events. AbbVie attributed the decline in Humira sales in 2025 to biosimilar competition.</p>
