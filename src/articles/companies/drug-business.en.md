Mounjaro and Zepbound have different brand names and the same active ingredient, tirzepatide. In the U.S., Mounjaro was approved to improve blood sugar control in people with type 2 diabetes. Zepbound was approved for long-term weight management in adults who meet specific criteria. When a drug makes the news, start with the brand and active ingredient, then check which use was approved for that brand. [FDA announcement of Zepbound’s first approval](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-new-medication-chronic-weight-management)

<h2 id="drug-milestones">Where to check patent, approval and launch dates</h2>

A **patent** protects a particular invention, such as an active ingredient, a formulation, or a use. U.S. law also provides **regulatory exclusivity**, which under certain conditions restricts when applications for competing products can be submitted or approved. The two forms of protection can cover different things and run for different periods. [FDA questions and answers on patents and exclusivity](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-approval-process-drugs/frequently-asked-questions-patents-and-exclusivity)

**Generics** and **biosimilars** go through separate FDA approval pathways. A generic generally has the same active ingredient as the reference drug and demonstrates that it delivers that ingredient to the body in an equivalent way. A biosimilar has to be highly similar to the reference biologic, with no clinically meaningful differences. The FDA’s Orange Book lists applicable patents and exclusivities for the drugs it covers. The Purple Book lists licensed biologics and their approved biosimilars. [FDA generic approval process](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/generic-drugs/what-approval-process-generic-drugs) · [FDA biosimilar overview](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/biosimilars/biosimilars-basics-patients) · [Orange Book preface](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-approval-process-drugs/orange-book-preface) · [Purple Book search](https://purplebooksearch.fda.gov/)

**Amjevita**, a biosimilar to Humira, received FDA approval in September 2016. The following year Amgen and AbbVie reached a settlement over the patent dispute, and Amgen announced plans for a January 2023 U.S. launch. The product became available in the U.S. on January 31, 2023. [FDA approval record](https://www.fda.gov/media/161123/download) · [Amgen’s 2017 settlement announcement](https://investors.amgen.com/node/14666/pdf) · [Amgen U.S. launch announcement](https://investors.amgen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/amjevitatm-adalimumab-atto-first-biosimilar-humirar-now)

Samsung Bioepis’s **Hadlima**, another Humira biosimilar, first received U.S. approval in July 2019. Samsung Bioepis and its sales partner Organon announced the U.S. launch in July 2023. [Hadlima’s first approval in the FDA Purple Book](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/purplebooksearch/index.cfm?blaNo=761059&event=productdetails) · [Samsung Bioepis and Organon launch announcement](https://www.organon.com/news/organon-samsung-bioepis-announce-us-launch-of-humira-biosimilar-hadlima-adalimumab-bwwd-in-multiple-presentations-consistent-with-originator/)

## How much of the business comes from one drug?

- **Eli Lilly** shows Mounjaro and Zepbound on separate lines in its 2025 annual report. The table shows where each brand of the same active ingredient generates revenue. [Form 10-K for the year ended December 2025, revenue by product](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)
- At **Merck**, Keytruda and Keytruda Qlex together accounted for **about 49%** of total sales in 2025. When a product line accounts for that much, competing launches or changes in approved uses can affect the whole business. [Form 10-K for the year ended December 2025, product and consolidated sales](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/310158/000031015826000063/R47.htm)
- **AbbVie’s** worldwide Humira net revenue dropped from about **$9.0 billion** in 2024 to **$4.5 billion** in 2025. AbbVie pointed to biosimilar competition as the main reason. The same product table shows how sales of Skyrizi and Rinvoq grew over the period. [Form 10-K for the year ended December 2025, net revenue by product and discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1551152/000155115226000008/abbv-20251231.htm)

Check what the company means when it reports a product’s performance. South Korean drugmaker Hanmi, for example, cites domestic prescription data in a release about Rosuzet. To work out a product’s share of company sales, use revenue for the product and the whole company covering the same period and market. [Hanmi’s release on prescription figures in Korean](https://www.hanmi.co.kr/about/news-media/press/detail-2282.hm)
