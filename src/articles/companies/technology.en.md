<h2>Where does an endpoint subscription go?</h2>

<p>A company can pay <a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/crowdstrike/">CrowdStrike</a> for Falcon protection on employee laptops. The sensors send signals to a platform that must run somewhere. CrowdStrike identifies AWS as a third-party hosting provider and also runs infrastructure at colocation facilities.</p>

<p>The subscription is the software seller’s revenue. Hosting is one of its expenses, and the buyer’s invoice does not reveal how much went to AWS. An ad in a free app follows a different path: an advertiser bids for an impression or an outcome. Meta reports impressions delivered and average price per ad, rather than cloud usage.</p>

<h2>The 13 companies in this sector</h2>

<div class="roster">

<div><h3>Devices, storage and networks</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/apple/">Apple</a><small>AAPL</small></b><span>Sells iPhones, Macs and accessories, alongside services such as device support, digital content and cloud storage.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/seagate/">Seagate Technology</a><small>STX</small></b><span>Sells high-capacity disk drives to data-center and other buyers that need persistent, economical storage.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/cisco/">Cisco</a><small>CSCO</small></b><span>Sells networking hardware and software that connect branch offices, enterprise campuses and data centers, alongside security tools and support.</span></li>

</ul></div>

<div><h3>Cloud infrastructure and computing</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/amazon/">Amazon</a><small>AMZN</small></b><span>AWS charges for computing, storage and database services. Retail sales, third-party seller fees and advertising are separate businesses.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/microsoft/">Microsoft</a><small>MSFT</small></b><span>Rents cloud infrastructure through Azure and licenses productivity, security and business software to organizations and individuals.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/alphabet/">Alphabet</a><small>GOOGL</small></b><span>Sells ad placements in Search and YouTube. Google Cloud charges for computing, data services and business applications.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/oracle/">Oracle</a><small>ORCL</small></b><span>Licenses database software and sells cloud infrastructure and business applications, including support for existing installations.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/ionq/">IonQ</a><small>IONQ</small></b><span>Sells quantum systems and access to quantum computing capacity, including through cloud marketplaces.</span></li>

</ul></div>

<div><h3>Subscriptions, data and security</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/crowdstrike/">CrowdStrike</a><small>CRWD</small></b><span>Sells subscriptions to Falcon modules that collect and analyze security signals from endpoints, identities and cloud workloads.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/palo-alto-networks/">Palo Alto Networks</a><small>PANW</small></b><span>Sells network security appliances and software, cloud security and security operations tools. Its installed products can generate recurring subscriptions and support fees.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/palantir/">Palantir</a><small>PLTR</small></b><span>Licenses platforms that combine data from separate systems and let government or commercial users act on it through applications.</span></li>

</ul></div>

<div><h3>Advertising platforms</h3><ul>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/meta/">Meta Platforms</a><small>META</small></b><span>Sells advertisers access to audiences on apps such as Facebook and Instagram. Advertisers can pay for impressions or other results.</span></li>

<li><b><a href="/en/blog/companies/technology/applovin/">AppLovin</a><small>APP</small></b><span>Sells technology for bidding on ad inventory and measuring campaigns, and helps publishers monetize available advertising space.</span></li>

</ul></div>
