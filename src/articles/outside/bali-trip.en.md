We wanted to see both the forest and the sea in Bali, so we booked two hotels: **Padma Resort Ubud** north of town and **Alila Seminyak** beside Petitenget Beach. Once those pins were on the map, we started looking at what might fit around them, from a sunrise in the mountains to a surf lesson before flying home. These are the notes we made while planning.

The word that kept popping up in our surfing notes? Shower. If we were going to get in the ocean after checkout, we wanted somewhere to rinse off before heading to the airport.

We planned to head into the hills from the airport, then move down to the coast. Seeing the pins on a map helped. Padma Resort Ubud sits in Payangan, a fair way north of central Ubud if you’re planning several trips into town. [Driving directions between the hotels](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Padma+Resort+Ubud+Payangan+Bali&destination=Alila+Seminyak+Bali&travelmode=driving)

<h2 id="kintamani">What we looked into at Mount Batur</h2>

We had three scenes in mind for Kintamani: the sky lighting up over the mountain, the dark lava fields, and a soak beside Lake Batur. The sunrise hike and the jeep outing are separate ways to see the area. With the jeep option, it helps to ask where the driver stops and whether the black lava fields are part of the route. [Indonesia Travel · Kintamani](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/travel-ideas/adventure/tips-1-hari-di-kintamani-bali-dari-sunrise-sampai-sunset) · [Find Kintamani on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kintamani+Bangli+Bali)

**Toya Devasya** was one lakeside hot spring we looked into. Putting it between the airport and hotel check-in raised two questions: would there be room for our suitcases, and could the driver take us on to Payangan? We started with sunrise photos and ended up thinking about luggage. [Toya Devasya](https://toyadevasya.com/home-hotspring/) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Toya+Devasya+Bali)

<h2 id="payangan">Our first base in the forests of Payangan</h2>

Padma Resort Ubud overlooks a valley north of Ubud. The forest-facing pool in the resort photos looked like a good place for a slow day. We also looked into the **Jungle BBQ**, an outdoor dinner on the lawn by the pool, and made a note to check what happens if it rains. [Padma Resort Ubud](https://padmaresortubud.com/) · [Jungle BBQ](https://padmaresortubud.com/dining/jungle-bbq.php) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Padma+Resort+Ubud+Payangan+Bali)

<h2 id="seminyak">Heading for the beach in Seminyak</h2>

The second hotel we booked, **Alila Seminyak**, opens onto Petitenget Beach. We made a note of the hotel’s **Beach Bar** for the sea view and **Warung Nia**, which serves Balinese dishes and pork ribs, as a lunch option around check-in. [Alila Seminyak](https://www.hyatt.com/alila-hotels-and-resorts/en-US/dpsas-alila-seminyak/faqs) · [Beach Bar](https://www.hyatt.com/alila-hotels-and-resorts/en-US/dpsas-alila-seminyak/dining/beach-bar) · [Warung Nia](https://warungnia.com/) · [Find Warung Nia on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Warung+Nia+Seminyak+Bali)

The last-day beginner surf lessons we looked into were around **Double Six Beach**. We checked where the lessons would meet and how far that was from the hotel. Our notes soon included where to leave the bags and where to change out of wet clothes before the evening flight. [Indonesia Travel · Seminyak](https://www.indonesia.travel/ph/en/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali/seminyak-the-truly-best-experiences-on-the-enchanting-island-of-bali) · [Find Double Six Beach on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Double+Six+Beach+Seminyak+Bali)
