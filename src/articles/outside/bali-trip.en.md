## Mount Batur and the hot springs in Kintamani

We looked into going from the airport to Kintamani before checking in. Mount Batur has a sunrise trek on foot and jeep trips to viewpoints. Our notes paired a jeep viewpoint with the black lava fields and left a hot spring stop open if there was time. One of the options was Toya Devasya, beside Lake Batur. [Indonesia’s tourism guide to Kintamani](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/travel-ideas/adventure/tips-1-hari-di-kintamani-bali-dari-sunrise-sampai-sunset) · [Toya Devasya hot springs](https://toyadevasya.com/home-hotspring/) · [Kintamani on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kintamani+Bangli+Bali)

For an airport-to-hotel route via Kintamani, ask where the driver can meet you, which vehicle will carry your suitcases, and whether the trip can end at your hotel. Compare the actual jeep viewpoint and whether the lava fields and hot springs are included. [Example jeep itinerary](https://mountbatur-jeeptour.id/jeep-tour-kintamani/)

## Padma Resort Ubud in Payangan

Padma Resort Ubud is in Payangan, north of central Ubud. It sits by a valley, with an infinity pool looking out towards the forest. If you want to walk to restaurants and markets in town, the pin on the map is more useful than the place name in the hotel’s title. [Padma Resort Ubud](https://padmaresortubud.com/) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Padma+Resort+Ubud+Payangan+Bali)

We also asked about the resort’s Jungle BBQ, an outdoor dinner on the lawn by the pool. Our notes included an alternative in case of rain. For an outdoor dinner, it helps to know which evenings it runs and what happens when it rains. [Padma’s Jungle BBQ](https://padmaresortubud.com/dining/jungle-bbq.php)

## Alila Seminyak and Warung Nia

Our second booking was Alila Seminyak, with direct access to Petitenget Beach. The change of base takes you from a valley to the sea. We set aside time around the beach and the hotel’s Beach Bar, which looks out over the Indian Ocean. [Alila Seminyak guide](https://www.hyatt.com/alila-hotels-and-resorts/en-US/dpsas-alila-seminyak/faqs) · [Beach Bar](https://www.hyatt.com/alila-hotels-and-resorts/en-US/dpsas-alila-seminyak/dining/beach-bar) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Alila+Seminyak+Bali)

**Warung Nia** was our lunch idea for the day we switched hotels. It serves Balinese dishes and barbecue pork ribs in central Seminyak. Having a lunch option near the new hotel gave us somewhere to aim for around check-in. [Warung Nia](https://warungnia.com/) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Warung+Nia+Seminyak+Bali)

## Surfing, showers and the last trip to the airport

We looked at beginner surf lessons around Seminyak and Double Six for the last day. A massage and sunset by the beach were also on the list. The detail that kept coming up in the trip notes was the shower. If you surf after checkout, you still need somewhere to change and deal with your wet clothes before collecting your bags. [Indonesia’s tourism guide to Seminyak](https://www.indonesia.travel/ph/en/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali/seminyak-the-truly-best-experiences-on-the-enchanting-island-of-bali) · [Double Six Beach on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Double+Six+Beach+Seminyak+Bali)

If we planned this route again, these are the three things we would check together.

- **Before booking a surf lesson**, confirm the meeting beach and where you can shower and change. Check the beach pin against the hotel on a map.
- **Before checking out**, ask the hotel about storing luggage and using a shower separately. You may need both after handing back the room key.
- **Before heading to the airport**, leave time to get changed and pick up your bags. Allow for the road conditions on the day. [Bali’s Ngurah Rai International Airport](https://hubud.kemenhub.go.id/bandara/11)

Two hotels mean an extra checkout and transfer. For our Bali itinerary, they gave the inland and coastal parts of the trip their own space. If you’re looking at Ubud and Seminyak together, pin both hotels on a map. The rest of the route starts to make more sense around them.
