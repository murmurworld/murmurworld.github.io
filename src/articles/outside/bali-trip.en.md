Looking at our Bali itinerary again, we were a little ambitious. A sunrise in the mountains, a couple of bases to linger in, and maybe a dip in the ocean before flying home. We booked **Padma Resort Ubud** in the forest north of town first, then **Alila Seminyak** beside Petitenget Beach.

The word that kept popping up in our surfing notes? Shower. If we were going to get in the ocean after checkout, we wanted somewhere to rinse off before heading to the airport.

The route took shape around a move from the hills down to the coast. Once I put both pins on a map, another detail stood out. The Padma has “Ubud” in its name, but it sits well north of town in Payangan. Planning several quick trips into central Ubud would have meant more time in the car. [Driving directions between the hotels](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Padma+Resort+Ubud+Payangan+Bali&destination=Alila+Seminyak+Bali&travelmode=driving)

<h2 id="kintamani">A sunrise above Mount Batur</h2>

We had three scenes in mind for Kintamani: the sky lighting up over the mountain, the dark lava fields, and a soak beside Lake Batur. The sunrise hike and the jeep outing are separate ways to see the area. With the jeep option, it helps to ask where the driver stops and whether the black lava fields are part of the route. [Indonesia Travel · Kintamani](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/travel-ideas/adventure/tips-1-hari-di-kintamani-bali-dari-sunrise-sampai-sunset) · [Find Kintamani on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kintamani+Bangli+Bali)

**Toya Devasya** was one lakeside hot spring we looked into. If you put this stretch between an airport arrival and hotel check-in, ask what vehicle can take your luggage and where the tour can drop you off. We started out looking at jeep photos and ended up working out where the suitcases would go. [Toya Devasya](https://toyadevasya.com/home-hotspring/) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Toya+Devasya+Bali)

<h2 id="payangan">Our first base in the forests of Payangan</h2>

Padma Resort Ubud overlooks a valley north of Ubud. With its pool facing the forest, it suited the part of the trip we wanted to spend near the hotel. We looked into the **Jungle BBQ**, an outdoor dinner on the lawn by the pool, and noted what to check if it rained. [Padma Resort Ubud](https://padmaresortubud.com/) · [Jungle BBQ](https://padmaresortubud.com/dining/jungle-bbq.php) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Padma+Resort+Ubud+Payangan+Bali)

<h2 id="seminyak">Heading for the beach in Seminyak</h2>

**Alila Seminyak** opens onto Petitenget Beach. Once we had that pin down, our searches ranged from the hotel’s beach bar to Balinese barbecue. **Warung Nia**, which serves local dishes and pork ribs, went into our notes as a lunch option around check-in. [Alila Seminyak](https://www.hyatt.com/alila-hotels-and-resorts/en-US/dpsas-alila-seminyak/faqs) · [Beach Bar](https://www.hyatt.com/alila-hotels-and-resorts/en-US/dpsas-alila-seminyak/dining/beach-bar) · [Warung Nia](https://warungnia.com/) · [Find Warung Nia on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Warung+Nia+Seminyak+Bali)

The beginner surf lessons we looked into were around **Double Six Beach**. The meeting beach matters, especially if you’re fitting a lesson in after leaving your hotel. We soon had another itinerary to plan inside the itinerary: where to leave the bags, where to change, and where to put the wet clothes before the flight. [Indonesia Travel · Seminyak](https://www.indonesia.travel/ph/en/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali/seminyak-the-truly-best-experiences-on-the-enchanting-island-of-bali) · [Find Double Six Beach on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Double+Six+Beach+Seminyak+Bali)
